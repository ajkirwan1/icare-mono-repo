## Status

**BLOCKED** - Requires compliance-specialist agent (JOB 7) and DPIA completion.

## Purpose

Defines the platform's approach to safeguarding vulnerable adults in accordance with the Care Act 2014 and UK safeguarding framework.

## Scope

- Protection of elderly care receivers
- Protection of vulnerable adults
- Incident reporting procedures
- Escalation to Safeguarding Adults Boards (SAB)
- Staff/caregiver training requirements

## Required Sections

1. Policy Statement
2. Definitions
3. Roles and Responsibilities
4. Recognition of Abuse
5. Reporting Procedures
6. Escalation Pathways
7. Record Keeping
8. Training Requirements
9. Review and Audit

## Regulatory Requirements

- Care Act 2014
- Mental Capacity Act 2005
- Human Rights Act 1998
- Equality Act 2010
