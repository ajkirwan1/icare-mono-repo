> **DOCUMENT PURPOSE**: This document specifies the requirements for the platform's Terms of Service. It is NOT the final Terms of Service (which requires legal counsel drafting). This document informs legal counsel on what must be included to reflect the Introduction Agency model and founder decisions.

**REQUIREMENTS DEFINED** - Ready for legal counsel drafting.

Define legally binding agreement between the platform and users (care receivers, families, caregivers) that reflects:

## CRITICAL SECTION 1: Caregiver Terms of Service

### Purpose
Establish and document caregiver's self-employed status to:
- Avoid IR35 challenges (disguised employment)
- Prevent employment tribunal claims
- Support Introduction Agency classification for CQC purposes
- Clarify liability allocation

### Required Content

#### 1.1 Self-Employment Declaration

**MUST include explicit statement**:

> "You acknowledge and agree that you are joining the Platform as a **self-employed independent professional**, NOT as an employee, worker, or agent of [Platform Name]. You are responsible for your own tax, National Insurance, and business obligations."

#### 1.2 Caregiver Responsibilities as Self-Employed Professional

**MUST specify**:

- **Own Professional Liability Insurance**: Caregiver is responsible for obtaining and maintaining Public Liability Insurance (minimum £1M) and Professional Indemnity Insurance (minimum £1M)
- **Own Tax and National Insurance**: Caregiver is responsible for registering with HMRC, paying self-assessment tax, and making National Insurance contributions
- **Full Control Over Care Delivery**: Caregiver determines how care is delivered (methods, timing, approach). Platform does NOT control care delivery.
- **Right to Accept/Decline Bookings**: Caregiver has absolute discretion to accept or decline any booking request. No penalty for declining.
- **Right to Set Own Rates**: Caregiver sets own hourly rate (within platform guidance range). Platform does NOT mandate rates.
- **Right to Arrange Substitutes**: Caregiver may arrange qualified, verified substitutes to fulfill bookings.
- **No Guaranteed Work**: Platform has NO obligation to offer bookings. Caregiver has NO obligation to accept bookings.

#### 1.3 What Platform Does NOT Provide

**MUST explicitly state Platform does NOT**:

- Provide employment benefits (holiday pay, sick pay, pension, maternity/paternity leave)
- Control care delivery methods or require specific procedures
- Assign or mandate bookings
- Guarantee minimum income or hours
- Provide equipment, uniforms, or tools (except platform software)
- Offer employment contract or worker status

#### 1.4 IR35 Supporting Language

**To support genuine self-employment**:

- **No Mutuality of Obligation**: "There is no obligation on the Platform to offer you work, and no obligation on you to accept work offered through the Platform."
- **No Control Over Work Performance**: "You determine how you deliver care services. The Platform does not supervise, direct, or control your care delivery methods."
- **Substitution Rights**: "You may arrange a qualified, verified substitute to fulfill bookings on your behalf, subject to Platform verification requirements and care receiver consent."

#### 1.5 Platform Role

**MUST clarify Platform provides**:

- Marketplace infrastructure connecting care receivers with caregivers
- Payment processing and escrow services
- Verification services (DBS, identity, qualifications, insurance)
- Safeguarding infrastructure (reporting tools, incident management)
- Quality signals (reviews, ratings, booking history)

**MUST clarify Platform does NOT**:

- Provide care services
- Employ caregivers
- Control care delivery
- Guarantee care quality or outcomes
- Create or manage clinical care plans

## CRITICAL SECTION 2: Care Receiver Terms of Service

### Purpose
Establish care receiver's understanding of Introduction Agency model and liability allocation.

### Required Content

#### 2.1 Platform Model Explanation

**MUST state**:

> "The Platform operates as an **Introduction Agency**. We connect you with self-employed professional caregivers. We do NOT employ caregivers, and we do NOT provide care services ourselves. Caregivers are independent professionals responsible for their own care delivery."

#### 2.2 Acknowledgments Required from Care Receiver

**MUST include**:

- **Caregivers are Self-Employed**: "You acknowledge that caregivers are self-employed professionals, NOT employees of the Platform."
- **Platform Does NOT Guarantee Care Quality**: "The Platform does NOT guarantee the quality of care provided by caregivers. Care quality is the responsibility of the individual caregiver you engage."
- **Platform Provides Verification, NOT Clinical Governance**: "The Platform verifies caregiver identity, DBS status, qualifications, and insurance. This verification does NOT constitute clinical oversight or guarantee of care competence."
- **Platform is NOT CQC-Registered**: "The Platform is NOT registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC). The Platform operates as an Introduction Agency connecting self-employed professionals."

#### 2.3 Care Receiver/Family Responsibilities

**MUST specify care receiver/family is responsible for**:

- **Communicating Care Needs**: Accurately communicating care needs, medical conditions, and care plan to caregiver
- **Providing Appropriate Environment**: Ensuring safe working environment for caregiver
- **Confirming Lawful Authority**: If booking on behalf of person lacking capacity, confirming you have lawful authority (Lasting Power of Attorney for Health and Welfare)
- **Paying for Services**: Paying for care services booked through the Platform
- **Own Care Decisions**: Making own decisions about which caregiver to engage and what care to request

#### 2.4 Mental Capacity Act Compliance

**MUST include** (if family account features implemented):

> "If you are booking care on behalf of someone who may lack mental capacity to consent to care arrangements, you confirm that:
> - You have lawful authority to make decisions on their behalf (Lasting Power of Attorney for Health and Welfare, or you are acting in their best interests under the Mental Capacity Act 2005), OR
> - The person consents to you booking care on their behalf."

## CRITICAL SECTION 3: Limitation of Liability

### Purpose
Protect Platform from liability for care quality, outcomes, and caregiver conduct.

### Required Content

#### 3.1 Platform IS Liable For

**Platform accepts liability for**:

- Platform safety (secure payment processing, data protection)
- Verification accuracy (accurate representation of verification status)
- Payment processing errors (incorrect charges, failed payouts)
- Safeguarding reporting obligations (Care Act 2014 duties)
- Data protection breaches (GDPR violations)

#### 3.2 Platform IS NOT Liable For

**Platform EXCLUDES liability for** (to maximum extent permitted by law):

- **Care Quality**: Quality of care provided by caregivers
- **Care Outcomes**: Health outcomes, care effectiveness, or suitability of care
- **Caregiver Conduct**: Actions, omissions, or negligence of caregivers during care delivery
- **Caregiver Qualifications**: Accuracy of caregiver-declared experience or skills (beyond verification of certificates provided)
- **Care Receiver Satisfaction**: Care receiver's satisfaction with caregiver or care services
- **Disputes Between Users**: Disputes between care receivers and caregivers
- **Loss or Damage**: Loss, damage, or injury arising from care delivery (unless caused by Platform negligence)

#### 3.3 Liability Cap

**Recommended**:

> "To the maximum extent permitted by law, Platform's total liability to you (whether in contract, tort, negligence, or otherwise) shall not exceed the total fees paid by you to the Platform in the 12 months preceding the claim."

**Exception**: No cap on liability for death or personal injury caused by Platform negligence, or fraud.

#### 3.4 Caregiver Liability

**MUST state**:

> "Caregivers are individually liable for their own professional conduct, care quality, and care outcomes. Caregivers are required to maintain Professional Indemnity and Public Liability insurance. Claims arising from care delivery should be directed to the caregiver, not the Platform."

## CRITICAL SECTION 4: Indemnification

### Purpose
Require caregivers and care receivers to indemnify Platform for claims arising from their actions.

### Required Content

#### 4.1 Caregiver Indemnification

**Caregiver agrees to indemnify Platform against**:

- Claims arising from caregiver's care delivery (negligence, injury, harm)
- Claims arising from caregiver's breach of self-employment obligations (HMRC challenges, employment tribunal claims)
- Claims arising from caregiver's false declarations (qualifications, experience, DBS status)
- Claims arising from caregiver's violation of Platform policies

#### 4.2 Care Receiver Indemnification

**Care receiver agrees to indemnify Platform against**:

- Claims arising from care receiver's misrepresentation of care needs or medical conditions
- Claims from third parties (family members) challenging care receiver's authority to book care
- Claims arising from unsafe working environment provided to caregiver
- Claims arising from care receiver's breach of payment obligations

## Additional Required Provisions

### Equality Act Compliance

**Gender Preference Policy**:

> "You may request a caregiver of a specific gender for legitimate reasons (e.g., religious or cultural requirements for personal care, dignity considerations). Such requests must be justified and proportionate. Discriminatory requests based on stereotypes or prejudice are prohibited and may result in account suspension."

### Safeguarding Obligations

**Platform's Safeguarding Duties** (Care Act 2014):

> "The Platform has safeguarding duties under the Care Act 2014. We will report safeguarding concerns to local authority Safeguarding Adults Boards where appropriate. We may share information with authorities where necessary to protect vulnerable adults, in accordance with GDPR and safeguarding law."

**User Reporting Obligations**:

> "If you witness or suspect abuse, neglect, or safeguarding concerns, you must report this immediately to the Platform using the safeguarding reporting feature. Failure to report safeguarding concerns may result in account suspension."

### Right to Work (Caregivers)

**Caregiver Declaration**:

> "You confirm you have the legal right to work in the UK. You will provide evidence of your right to work (UKVI share code or passport). If your right to work expires or your visa conditions restrict your working hours, you must notify the Platform immediately. Working beyond your visa restrictions is illegal and will result in immediate account termination."

### Payment Terms

**Escrow Model**:

> "Care receivers authorize payment when booking care. Funds are held in escrow by the Platform and released to the caregiver upon completion of care and confirmation by the care receiver. Platform charges a service fee [15%] to care receivers and a commission [10-15%] to caregivers."

### Cancellation Policy

**Care Receiver Cancellation**:
- 48+ hours before: Full refund minus service fee
- 24-48 hours: 50% refund
- <24 hours: No refund (caregiver compensated for late cancellation)

**Caregiver Cancellation**:
- 48+ hours before: Full refund to care receiver, no penalty to caregiver
- <48 hours: Full refund to care receiver, caregiver penalty/warning

**Emergency Exemptions**: Illness, family emergency (admin review)
