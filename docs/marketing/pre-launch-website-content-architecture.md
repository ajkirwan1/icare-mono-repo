## Document Purpose

This document defines the **information architecture and content requirements** for the pre-launch marketing website. It specifies what information must be presented to visitors, the messaging framework, and compliance requirements—without prescribing UI, visual design, or feature additions.

This is a **content-only specification** focused on validating demand, capturing leads, and building trust before the full product launches.

---

## 1. Site Purpose and Goals

### 1.1 Primary Purpose
Validate market demand for a UK elderly care marketplace connecting families with verified independent caregivers, while capturing early interest from both care receivers/families and professional caregivers.

### 1.2 Primary Goals
1. **Build Trust**: Establish credibility in the sensitive elderly care sector before the platform exists
2. **Capture Qualified Leads**: Collect contact information from care receivers/families and caregivers
3. **Validate Value Proposition**: Test messaging and positioning with target audiences
4. **Communicate Safety**: Clearly articulate safeguarding and verification commitments
5. **Set Expectations**: Manage launch timeline and service scope expectations

### 1.3 Success Metrics (Tracking Required)
- Email capture conversion rate (target: >15% for care receivers, >8% for caregivers)
- Source traffic analysis (organic, paid, referral)
- Time on site and scroll depth
- Geographic distribution of leads (UK postcode analysis)
- Care need types indicated (validation of medical condition focus)
- Caregiver qualification levels indicated

### 1.4 Anti-Goals (What This Site Is NOT)
- Not a functional marketplace (no browsing caregivers, no booking)
- Not collecting medical or sensitive personal data (beyond contact information and care needs categories)
- Not making promises about care quality or outcomes
- Not designed for scale (designed for validation)

- Subheadline: For professional caregivers seeking flexible, fairly-paid work

**Purpose:** Communicate unique value to caregivers

- Transparent, on-platform payments (no chasing invoices)
- Flexible scheduling (you control your availability)
- Fair compensation (you set your rates within platform guidelines)
- Professional recognition (showcase qualifications and experience)
- Support and safeguarding infrastructure
- No agency fees or long-term contracts

#### Section 3: Detailed Process Steps
**Required Content (5-7 steps):**

**Step 1: Create Your Professional Profile**
- Upload qualifications (Care Certificate, NVQ, nursing credentials)
- List medical condition experience (Parkinson's, dementia, etc.)
- Specify care skills (personal care, hoisting, dementia support, etc.)
- Set your hourly rate and service radius
- Add professional photo and bio

**Step 2: Complete Verification**
- DBS background check (Enhanced DBS preferred)
- ID and right-to-work verification
- Qualification verification
- Insurance verification (public liability required)
- Professional reference checks
- Timeline: Verification typically takes 5-10 business days

**Step 3: Set Your Availability**
- Define when you're available for bookings
- Block out holidays or personal time
- Update availability in real-time
- Control your schedule completely

**Step 4: Receive Booking Requests**
- Families send booking requests with care details
- Review care needs, location, date, and earnings
- Accept or decline based on your skills and availability
- 24-hour response window

**Step 5: Provide Care & Get Paid**
- Communicate with families through secure messaging
- Provide care as agreed
- Mark bookings complete after sessions
- Funds released to your bank account (2-3 business days)
- No chasing payments

**Visual Guidance:** Professional, respectful imagery of caregivers

#### Section 4: Who Can Join
**Purpose:** Set qualification expectations

**Required Content - Minimum Requirements:**
- Professional caregiving experience (minimum 1 year preferred)
- Relevant qualifications (Care Certificate, NVQ Level 2/3, or nursing background)
- Experience with elderly care
- Right to work in the UK
- Public liability insurance
- DBS check (or willing to obtain one)
- Professional references

**Note:** "We welcome caregivers from all backgrounds. If you have relevant experience but lack formal qualifications, please contact us."

#### Section 5: Verification & Compliance
**Purpose:** Set expectations about verification rigor

**Required Content:**
- All caregivers must complete verification before profile goes live
- Verification protects you and your clients
- Re-verification required periodically (DBS every 3 years, insurance annually)
- Platform compliance with UK employment and care regulations
- You remain self-employed (no IR35 concerns)

- You are free to work with other platforms or agencies simultaneously
- The platform provides safeguarding infrastructure and payment facilitation, but does not direct how you provide care

We recommend all caregivers register as self-employed with HMRC and seek independent tax advice."

**Guidance to Avoid IR35 Issues:**
- Emphasize caregiver autonomy (set rates, control schedule, accept/decline bookings)
- No requirement to work exclusively with the platform
- No direction on HOW care is provided (only that it must meet safety standards)
- Platform role is facilitation and safeguarding, not employment

## 9. Product/Content Gaps

### 9.1 Critical Product Decisions Required Before Launch

**GAP 1: Launch Timeline**
- **Content Impact:** Homepage hero, FAQ, Early Access pages, Thank You pages
- **Required Decision:** Specific launch date or realistic timeframe (e.g., "Q2 2026," "Summer 2026")
- **Recommendation:** If uncertain, use "Coming Soon" and set expectations that early access registrants will be notified first

**GAP 2: Launch Geography**
- **Content Impact:** FAQ, About Us, Early Access forms
- **Required Decision:** Initial service area (e.g., Greater London only, or nationwide from launch)
- **Recommendation:** Start with specific regions and expand (easier to manage demand and supply)

**GAP 3: Pricing Structure**
- **Content Impact:** FAQ, How It Works pages
- **Required Decision:**
  - Caregiver commission rate (platform takes X%)
  - Care receiver service fee (% or flat fee)
  - Typical hourly rate ranges for caregivers
  - Early adopter pricing (if any)
- **Recommendation:** Transparent pricing is critical for trust. If not finalized, state: "We're finalizing pricing and will share details with early access registrants before launch. Our goal is fair, transparent pricing for both families and caregivers."

**GAP 4: CQC Registration Status**
- **Content Impact:** Trust & Safety, About Us, FAQ, footer
- **Required Decision:**
  - Is the platform a regulated activity requiring CQC registration?
  - If yes, when will registration be obtained?
  - If no, clear rationale and alternative compliance measures
- **Recommendation:** Seek legal and regulatory advice URGENTLY. This is a compliance blocker. Until resolved, be transparent: "We are consulting with legal and regulatory advisors to determine our CQC registration requirements. Our safeguarding and verification commitments remain our top priority regardless of registration status."

**GAP 5: Caregiver Commission Rate**
- **Content Impact:** How It Works - Caregivers, FAQ
- **Required Decision:** Platform commission percentage (10-20% typical in marketplace models)
- **Recommendation:** Research competitor rates and caregiver feedback. Transparency is key. If not finalized: "Our commission will be competitive and transparent. We'll share the exact rate with caregivers who join early access."

**GAP 6: Brand Identity**
- **Content Impact:** All pages (logo, colors, tone)
- **Required Decision:**
  - Platform name (if not yet chosen)
  - Logo design
  - Color palette
  - Typography
- **Recommendation:** Can proceed with basic branding for pre-launch site, but final brand identity needed before full platform launch

**GAP 7: Early Adopter Incentives**
- **Content Impact:** Early Access pages
- **Required Decision:**
  - Do early access registrants get priority access? (Yes, recommend this)
  - Do they get discounted pricing? (Optional, but attractive)
  - Do caregivers get reduced commission initially? (Optional, could incentivize supply)
- **Recommendation:** At minimum, guarantee "priority access." Consider limited-time incentives to validate demand.

## 10. Implementation Recommendations

### 10.1 Technical Requirements

**Platform:**
- Static site (HTML/CSS/JS) or simple CMS (e.g., WordPress, Webflow)
- Fast page load (< 3 seconds)
- Mobile-responsive (majority of care sector users on mobile)
- HTTPS required (SSL certificate)

**Forms:**
- Form submission to email marketing platform (Mailchimp, SendGrid, ConvertKit)
- Confirmation emails (double opt-in)
- Form data stored securely (GDPR-compliant database or CRM)

**Analytics:**
- Google Analytics or privacy-focused alternative (Plausible, Fathom)
- Goal tracking (form submissions, page views, time on site)
- UTM parameter tracking (for paid ads, referrals)

**Hosting:**
- Reliable hosting (Netlify, Vercel, AWS S3 + CloudFront for static; or managed WordPress hosting)
- Backup and uptime monitoring

---

### 10.2 Content Delivery & Maintenance

**Content Management:**
- CMS for easy updates (FAQ, blog if applicable)
- Version control for legal pages (track changes to Privacy Policy, Terms)

**Email Communication:**
- Confirmation emails (immediate after form submission)
- Welcome sequence (3-5 emails over first 2 weeks):
  1. Confirmation + what to expect
  2. Why we're building this (mission, values)
  3. How you can help (feedback, referrals)
  4. Timeline update
  5. Next steps before launch
- Regular updates (monthly or bi-monthly) until launch

**Legal Compliance:**
- Legal review of Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy BEFORE site goes live
- Annual review of legal pages (or when regulations change)

### 10.3 Launch Sequence

**Phase 1: Soft Launch (Invite-Only)**
1. Share with personal networks, advisors
2. Gather feedback on messaging and clarity
3. Test form submissions and email delivery
4. Iterate based on feedback

**Phase 2: Public Launch**
1. Announce publicly (social media, press if applicable)
2. Paid ads (Google Ads, Facebook/Instagram, targeted to UK care seekers)
3. SEO optimization (meta tags, structured data, sitemap)
4. Partnerships (care organizations, charities, local authorities)

**Phase 3: Ongoing Optimization**
1. A/B testing (headlines, CTAs, form length)
2. Analyze conversion rates and drop-off points
3. Iterate based on data
4. Regular content updates (FAQ additions, blog posts)

---

### 10.4 Success Criteria

**Quantitative Metrics:**
- 500+ family/care receiver email leads in first 3 months
- 200+ caregiver email leads in first 3 months
- 15%+ conversion rate on early access forms
- 50%+ of leads from target geographic areas
- <5% email unsubscribe rate

**Qualitative Metrics:**
- Positive feedback on clarity and trust signals
- Questions in FAQ actually answering user concerns (low repeat inquiries)
- High-quality caregiver leads (qualified, DBS-ready)
- Care receiver leads with specific medical condition needs (validates matching feature)

**Red Flags to Monitor:**
- High bounce rate (>70%) = messaging not resonating or traffic source mismatch
- High unsubscribe rate (>10%) = email content not valuable or too frequent
- Low form completion rate (<10%) = form too long or trust issues
- Geographic mismatch (leads outside target areas) = targeting issues
