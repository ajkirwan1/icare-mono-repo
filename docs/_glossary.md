# Terminology Glossary - UK Elderly Care Marketplace

**Document Purpose**: Standardized terminology for consistent use across all documentation.

**Created**: 2026-02-01
**Owner**: Product Director
**Status**: Active

---

## User Roles

| Standard Term | Definition | Acceptable Variations | Do NOT Use |
|---------------|------------|----------------------|------------|
| **Care Receiver** | The elderly person receiving care services through the platform | Care receiver, care receiver (lowercase in sentences) | Client, customer, patient, service user (except in legal contexts) |
| **Family Member** | A person acting on behalf of a care receiver | Family, Family Booker, Proxy User | Relative, carer (this means caregiver in UK English) |
| **Caregiver** | Self-employed professional providing care services | Caregiver, caregiver (lowercase) | Carer (ambiguous in UK), care worker, care professional (except in marketing), employee |
| **Admin** | Platform operations staff | Admin, Administrator, Operations | Staff, employee |
| **Safeguarding Officer** | Admin with safeguarding responsibilities | Safeguarding Lead | - |

---

## Business Model Terms

| Standard Term | Definition | Context |
|---------------|------------|---------|
| **Introduction Agency** | Legal classification: Platform connects self-employed professionals with service users | Used in legal and regulatory contexts |
| **Self-employed** | Caregiver employment status: NOT employees of the platform | Always hyphenated: "self-employed" |
| **Platform** | The marketplace system including app and website | Preferred over "service", "product", "app" |
| **Marketplace** | Two-sided marketplace connecting supply (caregivers) and demand (care receivers) | Use when describing business model |

## Service Types

| Standard Term | Definition | Tier |
|---------------|------------|------|
| **Companionship** | Social interaction, conversation, activities | T1 |
| **Light housework** | Cleaning, tidying, laundry | T1 |
| **Shopping and errands** | Shopping assistance, prescription pickup | T1 |
| **Meal preparation** | Cooking meals (no feeding assistance at T1) | T1 |
| **Personal care** | Washing, dressing, toileting | T2+ |
| **Mobility assistance** | Walking support, transfers | T2+ |
| **Medication assistance** | Prompting only, non-clinical | T2+ |
| **Live-in care** | Overnight/residential care arrangement | T3+ |
| **Condition-specific matching** | Matching based on medical conditions | T3+ |
