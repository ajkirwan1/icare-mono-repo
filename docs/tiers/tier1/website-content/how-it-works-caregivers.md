## Getting Started: Step-by-Step

### Section Headline
**From Application to First Booking in 5-7 Days**

### Step 1: Create Your Profile

#### What You'll Build
Your professional caregiver profile is your shop window. You'll create:

**Personal Introduction**:
- Professional photo (friendly, clear headshot)
- Bio (300-500 words about your experience, approach to care, and what makes you a great caregiver)
- Services you offer (companionship, housework, shopping, meals)

**Experience**:
- Years in care/companionship work
- Types of settings you've worked in (private care, care homes, NHS, etc.)
- What you enjoy most about care work

**Practical Details**:
- Your hourly rate (you set this)
- Service radius (how far you'll travel: 5-30 miles)
- General availability pattern

**Tips for a Great Profile**:
- Use a warm, professional photo
- Write in your own voice - be authentic
- Highlight what makes you special
- Explain why you love companionship work
- Proofread for spelling and grammar

**Time to Complete**: 20-30 minutes

---

### Step 2: Complete Verification

#### Mandatory Verification (All Caregivers)

**Government ID Verification**:
- Completed through Stripe Identity (secure, industry-standard)
- Upload your passport or driving license
- Take a quick selfie to confirm photo match
- System verifies document authenticity
- Usually instant approval, sometimes up to 24 hours

**Right to Work Verification**:
- UK citizens: Passport or birth certificate
- Non-UK nationals: UKVI share code (from gov.uk)
- Our admin team manually checks each submission
- Usually reviewed within 1-2 business days

**Phone Verification**:
- SMS code sent to your mobile
- Confirms you have access to the number provided

**Email Verification**:
- Confirmation link sent to your email address

---

#### Voluntary Verification (Optional but Recommended)

**DBS Certificate Upload**:

Companionship services (our current offering) are **not legally classified as "regulated activity"**, so DBS checks are not mandatory at this stage.

**However**: If you have an existing DBS certificate (Enhanced DBS preferred), you can upload it for verification. This earns you a **"DBS Verified"** badge on your profile, which many families value highly.

To upload:
- Provide certificate PDF or clear photo
- Include certificate number and issue date
- Our admin team verifies authenticity
- Badge added to your profile once confirmed

**Why consider uploading even though it's optional?**
- Families can filter to show only DBS Verified caregivers
- It builds additional trust
- You'll be ahead of the game when DBS becomes mandatory for personal care services

**When Personal Care Launches**: DBS will become **mandatory** for all caregivers offering personal care. If you upload now, you'll be ready to offer those services immediately when they launch.

---

#### What Happens During Verification

**Timeline**:
- ID verification: Instant to 24 hours
- Right to work check: 1-2 business days
- DBS verification (if uploaded): 2-3 business days
- Total typical timeline: 3-5 days

**Admin Review**:
Once all verification is complete, our admin team manually reviews your profile:
- Checks all verification is valid
- Reviews profile content for appropriateness
- Ensures bio and services are clear
- Looks for any red flags

**Approval or Feedback**:
- If approved: Your profile goes live and you can start receiving booking requests
- If changes needed: We'll contact you with specific feedback to improve your profile

---

### Step 3: Set Availability and Rates

#### Your Availability Calendar

Control exactly when you're available for bookings:

**Ongoing Availability**:
- Set regular weekly patterns (e.g., "Available Tuesdays and Thursdays 10am-4pm")
- Block out personal commitments
- Set general availability that families can see when browsing

**Specific Availability**:
- Mark specific dates/times as available or unavailable
- Update in real-time if your schedule changes
- Families only see your general pattern, not your exact availability until they request a booking

**No Penalties for Being Unavailable**:
- There's no requirement to be available a minimum amount
- Work as much or as little as you want
- Update availability anytime

---

#### Setting Your Hourly Rate

**You Decide Your Rate**:

Based on:
- Your experience and qualifications
- Local market rates in your area
- Services you offer
- Your availability and flexibility

**Market Context**:
- Companionship caregivers typically charge: 12-25 GBP/hour
- More experienced caregivers: 18-25 GBP/hour
- Less experienced: 12-18 GBP/hour
- London/Southeast often higher than other regions

**Platform Commission**:
- iCare charges a 10% commission on each booking
- This covers payment processing, verification, safeguarding infrastructure, and platform maintenance
- Commission is deducted before payout, so you receive 90% of your advertised rate

**Example**:
- Your rate: 20 GBP/hour
- 3-hour booking: 60 GBP
- Platform commission (10%): 6 GBP
- You receive: 54 GBP

**You can change your rate anytime**, but existing booking requests honor the rate advertised at the time.

---

#### Setting Your Service Radius

How far will you travel for bookings?

Options:
- 5 miles (very local)
- 10 miles (local area)
- 15 miles (broader local)
- 20 miles (regional)
- 30 miles (wide area)

Families searching by postcode will only see you if they're within your service radius. Consider:
- Travel time
- Transport costs
- Whether you want local-only or are happy to travel further

You can change your radius at any time.

---

### Step 4: Receive Booking Requests

#### How Booking Requests Work

**Notification**:
When a family requests a booking with you, you receive:
- In-app notification
- Email notification
- (Future: SMS/push notification)

**Request Details**:
- Family/care receiver name
- Date and time requested
- Duration (minimum 2 hours)
- Service type (companionship, housework, shopping, meal prep)
- Any special requests or notes
- Total amount you'll earn (after commission)
- Care receiver location (distance from you)

---

#### Your Response Options

**You Have 24 Hours to Respond**:

**Option 1: Accept**
- Booking is confirmed
- Payment is captured from the family and held in escrow
- You both receive contact details (phone numbers)
- Booking appears in your calendar
- You're committed to fulfilling the booking

**Option 2: Decline**
- No penalty or negative consequence
- Optionally provide a brief reason (schedule conflict, too far, outside comfort zone, etc.)
- Family is notified and can request another caregiver
- You remain available for other requests

**Option 3: No Response (Auto-Decline)**
- If you don't respond within 24 hours, the request automatically declines
- Same as manually declining - no penalty
- Helps you if you forget to check the app

---

#### Why You Can Decline Without Penalty

**You're Self-Employed**:
As a self-employed professional, you have the right to choose which bookings to accept. This is a key part of self-employment status and gives you genuine control over your work.

Reasons caregivers decline:
- Schedule change or personal commitment
- Location too far or difficult to reach
- Prefer different type of booking
- Family's specific requests outside your comfort zone
- Already accepted another booking

**No Acceptance Quota**: There's no requirement to accept a certain percentage of requests. Quality over quantity.

#### Tax Responsibilities

**You Are Self-Employed**:

As a self-employed professional, you are responsible for:

**Income Tax**:
- Register with HMRC for Self Assessment
- Declare all income from iCare bookings
- Pay income tax on your earnings
- File annual tax return

**National Insurance**:
- Pay Class 2 and Class 4 National Insurance contributions
- Thresholds apply based on total annual earnings

**Record Keeping**:
- Keep records of all income
- Track business expenses (mileage, supplies, phone, etc.)
- Retain for 5 years

**iCare Does NOT**:
- Deduct tax from your payments
- Pay employer National Insurance
- Provide employment benefits (holiday pay, sick pay, pension)

**Resources**:
- HMRC Self Assessment: gov.uk/self-assessment-tax-returns
- We recommend consulting an accountant for tax advice
- Your earnings dashboard provides full payment history for tax records

## Self-Employment Status

### You Are NOT an Employee

**Important Legal Distinction**:

iCare is an **Introduction Agency**. We connect you with families who need companionship care. You are a **self-employed independent professional**, not an employee, worker, or agent of iCare.

### What This Means

**Control and Flexibility**:
- ✅ You set your own hourly rates
- ✅ You control your own schedule and availability
- ✅ You can accept or decline any booking request
- ✅ You decide how to deliver your care services
- ✅ You can work for other platforms or agencies simultaneously
- ✅ You can arrange qualified substitutes

**Financial Independence**:
- ✅ You are paid for services provided, not wages
- ✅ Platform commission covers platform services, not employment costs
- ✅ You are responsible for your own tax and National Insurance

**No Employment Relationship**:
- ❌ iCare does not control how you work
- ❌ iCare does not guarantee you work or minimum hours
- ❌ iCare is not your employer
- ❌ You do not receive employment benefits (holiday pay, sick pay, pension)
- ❌ You do not have employment rights against iCare

### Your Responsibilities

**Professional Service Delivery**:
- Provide professional, safe companionship care
- Maintain professional standards and conduct
- Honor bookings you accept
- Report safeguarding concerns

**Business Administration**:
- Register with HMRC for Self Assessment
- File annual tax returns
- Pay income tax and National Insurance
- Maintain appropriate insurance (recommended: public liability)
- Keep financial records

**Platform Compliance**:
- Follow iCare Terms of Service
- Maintain accurate profile information
- Keep verification up to date
- Use on-platform payments only (no cash or off-platform arrangements)
