## Introduction Agency Model

### Section Headline
**Understanding How iCare Works: The Introduction Agency Model**

### What is an Introduction Agency?

iCare is an **Introduction Agency**, not a care provider or care agency. This is an important legal distinction that affects how we operate and what we're responsible for.

#### What This Means

**We Connect, We Don't Provide**:
- iCare introduces you to **self-employed professional caregivers**
- Caregivers are **not employees** of iCare
- iCare does not control how caregivers deliver care
- iCare does not provide care services directly

**Your Choice, Your Engagement**:
- **You** choose which caregiver to book
- **You** engage the caregiver for services
- The caregiver is professionally responsible for the care they provide
- You have a direct relationship with the caregiver

#### What iCare Provides

**Platform Services**:
- ✅ Caregiver identity and right to work verification
- ✅ Secure payment processing and escrow
- ✅ Safeguarding reporting infrastructure
- ✅ Admin oversight and profile approval
- ✅ Booking coordination and messaging
- ✅ Review and rating system
- ✅ 24/7 incident reporting mechanisms

**What iCare Does NOT Provide**:
- ❌ Direct care services
- ❌ Employment of caregivers
- ❌ Control over how caregivers work
- ❌ Guaranteed care outcomes
- ❌ Clinical or medical services

#### Why This Model Matters

**For Families**:
- You have choice and control over who provides care
- You can review profiles, read reviews, and make informed decisions
- You work directly with caregivers to arrange care that suits your needs

**For Caregivers**:
- Professional autonomy - set own rates, control schedule
- Build independent businesses
- Flexibility and control

**For Platform Safety**:
- Clear responsibilities and accountability
- Focus on verification, payment protection, and safeguarding reporting
- Honest about our role and limitations

## Verification at Tier 1 (Our Current Stage)

### Section Headline
**Progressive Trust: Starting Safe, Getting Safer**

### Our Philosophy

We're launching with companionship services only and building verification progressively. We believe in:

- **Transparency**: Being honest about what verification we require at each stage
- **Legal Compliance**: Meeting all mandatory requirements for our service scope
- **Progressive Enhancement**: Adding verification as services expand
- **No Overc laims**: Only stating what we actually verify

---

### Mandatory Verification (All Caregivers, Tier 1)

#### 1. Government ID Verification

**What It Is**:
Every caregiver completes government identity verification using **Stripe Identity** or equivalent secure technology. This is the same verification system used by banks and financial institutions.

**What We Check**:
- Identity document is genuine (passport or driving license)
- Document has not been tampered with
- Photo on document matches live selfie taken during verification
- Name and date of birth are verified
- Document is current and valid

**Why It Matters**:
Confirms the caregiver is who they claim to be. No one can create a fake profile or impersonate someone else.

**Badge**: ✅ ID Verified

---

#### 2. Right to Work Verification

**What It Is**:
Every caregiver must prove they have legal permission to work in the UK.

**What We Check**:

**UK Citizens**:
- UK passport or birth certificate verification

**Non-UK Nationals**:
- UKVI share code (from gov.uk) confirming right to work
- Or relevant visa/permit documentation

**Manual Review**:
Our admin team manually checks every right to work submission. This is not automated.

**Why It Matters**:
Ensures all caregivers are legally permitted to provide care services in the UK. Protects families from unknowingly engaging someone without legal work permission.

**Badge**: ✅ Right to Work Verified

---

#### 3. Phone Verification

**What It Is**:
SMS code sent to caregiver's mobile phone. They must enter the code to verify they have access to the phone number provided.

**Why It Matters**:
- Confirms active, working phone number
- Enables emergency contact if needed
- Prevents disposable or fake numbers

---

#### 4. Email Verification

**What It Is**:
Confirmation link sent to caregiver's email address. They must click to verify.

**Why It Matters**:
- Confirms active email for platform communications
- Prevents fake email addresses
- Essential for notification and account security

---

#### 5. Manual Admin Approval

**What It Is**:
After all verification checks are complete, a real person on our admin team reviews every caregiver profile before it goes live.

**What We Review**:
- All verification documents are valid and complete
- Profile content is appropriate and professional
- Bio and service descriptions are clear and within scope
- No red flags or concerning information
- Profile photo is appropriate

**Quality Checks**:
- Grammar and professionalism
- Clarity of service offering
- Authenticity of profile

**No Automated Approval**:
Every single caregiver is reviewed by a human admin team member. We don't use automated approval algorithms.

**Why It Matters**:
Human oversight catches issues that automated systems miss. Ensures every caregiver meets our standards before they can offer services.

**Badge**: ✅ Admin Approved

---

### Voluntary Verification (Optional at Tier 1)

#### DBS Certificate Upload and Verification

**Important Context First**:

Companionship services (conversation, light housework, shopping, meal preparation) are **not legally classified as "regulated activity"** under the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006.

This means DBS background checks are **not legally required** for companionship-only caregivers.

**Why DBS is Voluntary at Tier 1**:
- Companionship is not personal care
- Legal framework distinguishes between companionship and regulated activity
- We're complying with the law, not cutting corners
- Many reputable companionship services operate without mandatory DBS

**However**: Many caregivers already have DBS certificates from other care work (care homes, domiciliary care, NHS, etc.). We allow caregivers to voluntarily upload existing DBS certificates for verification.

---

#### How Voluntary DBS Works

**Caregiver Uploads Certificate**:
- PDF or clear photo of DBS certificate
- Includes certificate number and issue date
- Preferably Enhanced DBS (the highest level)

**Our Admin Team Verifies**:
- Certificate appears genuine
- Certificate number and issue date are clearly visible
- Certificate is reasonably current (ideally within last 3 years, though DBS certificates don't technically expire)
- Disclosure level is appropriate (Enhanced preferred)

**If Verified**:
- Caregiver receives **"DBS Verified"** badge on their profile
- Badge is visible to all families searching caregivers
- Families can filter search results to show only DBS Verified caregivers

**What We DON'T Do (Yet)**:
- We don't check the DBS Update Service at Tier 1 (this is expensive infrastructure we'll add at Tier 2)
- We don't require all caregivers to obtain new DBS checks
- We don't mandate DBS for companionship services

---

#### Why Families Value DBS Even When Not Mandatory

Families often prefer caregivers with DBS checks because:
- Additional peace of mind
- Shows caregiver has been background-checked in the past
- Indicates experience in professional care settings
- Voluntary commitment to transparency

**You Can Choose**:
When searching for caregivers, you can filter to show only DBS Verified profiles if this is important to you. Or you can view all verified caregivers, knowing they've all completed ID, right to work, phone, email and admin approval checks.

---

#### When DBS Becomes Mandatory

**At Tier 2 (Personal Care Launch)**:

When we launch personal care services (washing, dressing, toileting, mobility assistance), DBS checks will become **mandatory** for all caregivers offering those services. Personal care IS classified as regulated activity, so DBS is legally required.

At that stage:
- ✅ Enhanced DBS mandatory for all personal care caregivers
- ✅ DBS Update Service checking (ongoing monitoring)
- ✅ Re-verification cycles
- ✅ Additional safeguarding training

**Timeline**: We estimate personal care services will launch in Tier 2, approximately 6-12 months after our Tier 1 launch, once we've validated the platform with companionship services.

**Badge Evolution**:
- Tier 1 Now: "DBS Verified" (voluntary badge)
- Tier 2 Future: "DBS Verified" (mandatory for personal care)

## Safeguarding Commitment

### Section Headline
**Care Act 2014 Compliance: Our Legal and Moral Duty**

### What is Safeguarding?

Safeguarding means protecting adults who are at risk of abuse or neglect. The **Care Act 2014** creates a legal duty for organizations working with vulnerable adults to have safeguarding policies and report concerns to local Safeguarding Adults Boards (SABs).

**iCare complies with Care Act 2014 safeguarding duties**, regardless of our Introduction Agency status or the fact that we're not CQC-registered.

---

### Our Safeguarding Infrastructure

#### 24/7 Incident Reporting System

**Always Available**:
- In-app "Report Concern" button on every caregiver profile and booking
- Email: safeguarding@icare-app.co.uk (monitored 24/7)
- Phone: [SAFEGUARDING HOTLINE] (24-hour response)

**How It Works**:
1. You report a concern via any channel
2. Incident logged immediately in our system
3. Safeguarding team notified (immediate for critical issues, 24-hour review for non-urgent)
4. Investigation begins
5. You receive acknowledgment within 24 hours
6. Action taken as appropriate
7. Outcome communicated where possible (some details may be confidential)

---

#### Admin Oversight and Intervention

**Continuous Monitoring**:
- Admin team reviews all flagged messages
- Monitors booking patterns for anomalies
- Investigates complaints and disputes
- Tracks caregiver ratings and reviews
- Identifies concerning behavior

**Powers to Act**:
- Suspend caregiver accounts pending investigation
- Suspend care receiver accounts if needed
- Ban users permanently for serious violations
- Escalate to police or SABs when appropriate
- Coordinate with emergency services

---

#### Emergency Escalation Procedures

**Immediate Danger**:

If you or your loved one are in immediate danger:

**1. Call 999 First**

Police and emergency services should always be your first call in an emergency. iCare is not an emergency response service.

**2. Then Notify iCare**

After ensuring immediate safety, contact our safeguarding team so we can:
- Suspend accounts if needed
- Cooperate with emergency services
- Document the incident
- Take platform-level action

---

#### Safeguarding Adults Board (SAB) Liaison

**What are SABs?**:
Every local authority in England has a Safeguarding Adults Board responsible for coordinating adult safeguarding in their area.

**Our Relationship with SABs**:
- We report serious concerns to relevant local SABs
- We cooperate with SAB investigations
- We follow SAB guidance and best practices
- We document all escalations and outcomes

**When We Escalate to SABs**:
- Suspected abuse or neglect of vulnerable adults
- Financial exploitation concerns
- Serious professional misconduct
- Patterns of concerning behavior
- Any situation where local authority involvement is appropriate

---

### What to Report

#### Types of Safeguarding Concerns

**Physical Abuse**:
- Hitting, slapping, pushing, restraining
- Rough handling or inappropriate force
- Any physical harm or injury

**Emotional/Psychological Abuse**:
- Verbal abuse, threats, intimidation
- Humiliation or belittling
- Isolation or controlling behavior
- Ignoring or withdrawing care as punishment

**Financial Exploitation**:
- Pressure to give money or gifts
- Unauthorized use of bank cards or accounts
- Theft of money or possessions
- Requests for off-platform payments (always report this)

**Neglect**:
- Failure to provide agreed care
- Ignoring care needs
- Not showing up for bookings without notice
- Leaving vulnerable adult in unsafe conditions

**Safeguarding Violations**:
- Off-platform payment requests (bypassing protection)
- Inappropriate personal relationships
- Boundary violations
- Professional misconduct

**Other Concerns**:
- Anything that makes you uncomfortable
- Behavior that seems unsafe
- Situations you're unsure about (we'd rather you report and we investigate than miss something important)

---

## Payment Protection

### Section Headline
**Secure Payments That Protect Everyone**

### How Our Payment System Protects You

#### Escrow System

**How It Works**:
1. When you book, payment is authorized on your card
2. Payment is captured and held in **escrow** (secure holding)
3. Funds are NOT released to caregiver yet
4. Care is provided
5. Booking marked as complete
6. Payment released to caregiver (2 business days)

**Why This Matters**:
- You don't pay until care is actually provided
- Caregivers are guaranteed payment once care is complete
- Platform can issue refunds if there's a problem
- No awkward conversations about money between you and caregiver

---

#### Secure Processing (Stripe)

All payments processed through **Stripe**, the industry-leading payment platform trusted by millions of businesses worldwide.

**Security Features**:
- Bank-level encryption
- PCI DSS compliance (highest payment security standard)
- Your card details never shared with caregivers
- Two-factor authentication
- Fraud detection and prevention

---

#### No Off-Platform Payments

**Platform Rule**: All payments must go through iCare.

**Why**:
- On-platform payments are protected by our Terms and escrow
- Off-platform payments (cash, direct bank transfer) bypass all protections
- If something goes wrong off-platform, we cannot help

**If a Caregiver Asks for Off-Platform Payment**:

🚨 **Report immediately** - This is a serious violation.

Caregivers are prohibited from requesting:
- Cash payments
- Direct bank transfers
- PayPal or other payment apps
- "Pay me directly to avoid the platform fee"

This is always a red flag and must be reported to our safeguarding team.

---

#### Refund Policy

**More than 24 hours before booking**: Full refund, no questions asked

**Less than 24 hours before booking**: 50% refund (partial compensation to caregiver for short-notice cancellation)

**No-show by family**: No refund (caregiver traveled and honored their commitment)

**No-show by caregiver**: Full refund + caregiver may face suspension

**Care not provided as agreed**: Contact support immediately - we investigate and can issue refunds

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is iCare CQC registered?

No. iCare is an Introduction Agency, not a care provider, so CQC registration is not required.

CQC regulates organizations that **provide care services directly**. We connect families with self-employed caregivers, but we don't provide care ourselves.

We do, however, comply with **Care Act 2014 safeguarding duties** which apply regardless of CQC status.

---

### Why is DBS voluntary for companionship services?

Because UK law distinguishes between companionship and personal care. Companionship services (conversation, light housework, shopping, meal prep) are **not "regulated activity"** under the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006, so DBS checks are not legally required.

However:
- Caregivers can voluntarily upload existing DBS for verification
- You can filter to show only DBS Verified caregivers
- DBS becomes mandatory when personal care services launch

We're being transparent about the legal framework rather than overstating our verification.

---

### How do you protect vulnerable adults without mandatory DBS?

Multiple layers of protection:

1. **ID Verification** - confirms identity (Stripe Identity, bank-level security)
2. **Right to Work** - confirms legal permission to work
3. **Manual Admin Approval** - human review of every caregiver
4. **Voluntary DBS Option** - available for caregivers who have certificates
5. **24/7 Safeguarding Reporting** - always available
6. **Reviews and Ratings** - peer feedback from families
7. **Escrow Payments** - payment protection
8. **Admin Oversight** - continuous monitoring
9. **SAB Liaison** - escalation to authorities when needed

Verification is important, but it's one piece of a larger safeguarding system.

---

### What if there's an emergency during a visit?

**Medical Emergency**: Call 999 first, then notify iCare

**Safeguarding Emergency**: Call 999 if immediate danger, then report to iCare safeguarding team

**Non-Emergency Concern**: Report via in-app button or email safeguarding@icare-app.co.uk

---

### Who is responsible if something goes wrong?

**Introduction Agency Model Means**:

- **Caregiver**: Professionally responsible for care they provide
- **iCare**: Responsible for verification, payment processing, safeguarding reporting infrastructure
- **Family**: Responsible for choosing appropriate caregiver and coordinating care

This is different from a care agency where the agency employs caregivers and is responsible for care quality.

---

### How do I report a concern?

**In-App**: "Report Concern" button on profiles and bookings

**Email**: safeguarding@icare-app.co.uk (24/7 monitoring)

**Phone**: [SAFEGUARDING HOTLINE] (24-hour response)

All reports taken seriously. Response within 24 hours for non-urgent, immediate for urgent concerns.

---

### Can I remain anonymous when reporting?

Yes, but it may limit our ability to investigate fully or keep you updated on outcomes. We balance confidentiality with investigation effectiveness.

---

### What happens to caregivers who violate policies?

Depending on severity:
- Warning and re-training (minor issues)
- Temporary suspension (pending investigation)
- Permanent ban (serious violations)
- Report to police or SABs (safeguarding concerns)
- All documented in audit logs
