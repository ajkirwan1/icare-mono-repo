## Our Story

### Section Headline
**How iCare Began**

### The Problem We Saw

Too many elderly adults experience loneliness and isolation, struggling to find reliable companionship and support. Traditional care agencies can be expensive, inflexible, and impersonal. Families often don't know where to start when looking for help.

At the same time, many professional caregivers feel undervalued, overworked, and underpaid by traditional agencies. They want to provide quality care but lack control over their schedules and earnings.

Existing online platforms often prioritize growth over safety, treating vulnerable adults like any other marketplace category.

**We knew there had to be a better way.**

---

### Our Approach

We're building iCare differently:

**Starting with Companionship**:
We're launching with companionship services only - conversation, light housework, shopping, meal preparation. We're starting with what we can verify safely and building progressively toward personal care and specialized services.

**Progressive Verification**:
We're honest about where we are. At Tier 1, we offer ID verification, right to work checks, admin approval, and voluntary DBS. As we expand to personal care, we'll add mandatory DBS, qualification verification, and insurance checks. We're building trust step by step.

**Introduction Agency Model**:
We don't employ caregivers or provide care directly. We connect families with self-employed professional caregivers, provide verification and payment infrastructure, and ensure robust safeguarding. This gives caregivers flexibility while giving families choice and transparency.

**Safety as Foundation**:
Every product decision is filtered through one question: "Does this protect vulnerable adults?" We comply with Care Act 2014 safeguarding duties. We have 24/7 incident reporting. We work with local Safeguarding Adults Boards. Safety isn't a feature - it's our foundation.

**Transparent Pricing**:
Caregivers set their own rates. Families see exactly what they'll pay. Platform charges a straightforward 10% commission to cover verification, payments, and safeguarding infrastructure. No hidden fees, no surprises.

## Our Values

### Section Headline
**What Drives Us Every Day**

### 1. Safety First, Always

**Vulnerable adults deserve protection, not just convenience.**

We prioritize safeguarding over growth. We build verification progressively. We comply with Care Act 2014. We invest in admin oversight and 24/7 incident reporting. We escalate to Safeguarding Adults Boards when needed.

If a feature compromises safety, we don't ship it. Period.

---

### 2. Dignity and Respect

**Every person deserves care that honors their humanity.**

Care receivers are people with rich life histories, preferences, and autonomy. Caregivers are skilled professionals, not servants. Our language, design, and policies reflect respect for both.

We avoid patronizing language. We showcase authentic photos, not staged stock imagery. We design for accessibility and cultural sensitivity. We celebrate the caregiver-care receiver relationship as a partnership.

---

### 3. Radical Transparency

**Honesty builds more trust than marketing spin.**

We're honest about:
- What services we offer now vs. what's coming
- What verification we require vs. what's voluntary
- What we're responsible for vs. what caregivers are responsible for
- Our Introduction Agency model (we connect, we don't provide care)
- Our progressive approach to building trust

We don't overpromise. We don't hide behind jargon. We explain complex regulations in plain language.

---

### 4. Empowerment for Caregivers

**Professional caregivers deserve control and fair pay.**

Caregivers are self-employed professionals, not employees we control. They:
- Set their own rates
- Choose their own schedules
- Accept or decline bookings freely
- Build their own professional reputations
- Keep 90% of what they earn

We provide infrastructure (verification, payments, safeguarding), not control.

---

### 5. Quality Over Scale

**We'd rather serve 100 families safely than 10,000 families recklessly.**

We're not chasing unicorn valuations or hypergrowth. We're building a sustainable, responsible business that serves vulnerable adults. We'll grow as fast as we can do so safely, and no faster.

We won't compromise verification to onboard caregivers faster. We won't skip safeguarding features to launch sooner. We won't cut corners to hit revenue targets.

## Compliance and Regulation

### Section Headline
**How We Operate Within UK Law**

### Introduction Agency Model

iCare operates as an **Introduction Agency** under UK law. This means:
- We connect families with self-employed caregivers
- We do NOT provide care services directly
- We are NOT a CQC-registered care provider (CQC registration is not required for Introduction Agencies)
- We DO comply with Care Act 2014 safeguarding duties

**Legal Opinions**:
We have obtained legal advice confirming our Introduction Agency status and our regulatory obligations. We operate within established legal frameworks.

---

### Care Act 2014 Safeguarding

We comply with **Care Act 2014** safeguarding duties:
- Designated safeguarding lead
- Clear safeguarding policies and procedures
- 24/7 incident reporting mechanisms
- Cooperation with local Safeguarding Adults Boards (SABs)
- Duty to report concerns to appropriate authorities

---

### GDPR and Data Protection

We are registered with the **Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)** as a data controller.

**Tier 1 Data Processing**:
- We collect standard personal data only (NO special category health data)
- We have completed a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)
- We process data lawfully under GDPR (contract performance, legitimate interest, consent)
- We respect data subject rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability)

As we expand to Tier 2+ and process health data, we will update our DPIA and obtain explicit consent where required.

---

### Voluntary CQC Alignment

While we're not CQC-registered (and don't need to be as an Introduction Agency), we voluntarily align with CQC good practice guidance where appropriate:
- Safeguarding policies
- Dignity and respect principles
- Person-centered care values
- Quality assurance practices

We monitor CQC guidance and may choose to pursue registration in future tiers if commercially beneficial and aligned with our model.
