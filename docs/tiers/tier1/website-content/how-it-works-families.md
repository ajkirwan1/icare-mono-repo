## Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Create Your Profile

#### What You'll Need
Creating your profile takes about 5 minutes. You'll provide:

- Your name, email and phone number
- Postcode (to find caregivers near you)
- Emergency contact information
- Basic information about the companionship support needed

#### Family Member Registration
If you're arranging care for an elderly relative, you can register as a family member and create a profile on their behalf. We understand that many families coordinate care together.

#### Privacy and Security
Your information is protected under GDPR. We only collect the minimum information needed to connect you with appropriate caregivers. We never share your contact details until you accept a booking.

**Time to Complete**: 5 minutes

---

### Step 2: Search for Caregivers

#### Location-Based Search
Search for caregivers in your local area by entering your postcode. Choose a search radius (5, 10, 15, 20 or 30 miles) to see caregivers who can travel to you.

#### Filter Your Search
Refine your search using these filters:

**Hourly Rate**: Set your budget range to see caregivers within your price expectations (typical range: 12-25 GBP per hour)

**Availability**: Filter by day of week and time of day to find caregivers whose schedule matches yours

**Gender Preference**: Some families prefer male or female caregivers - we respect that choice

**DBS Verified**: Filter to show only caregivers who have voluntarily uploaded and had verified DBS certificates (while DBS is not legally required for companionship, this provides additional peace of mind)

#### What You'll See on Caregiver Profiles

Each caregiver profile shows:

- Professional photograph
- Personal bio and introduction
- Services they offer (companionship, housework, shopping, meal prep)
- Hourly rate they've set
- Distance from your location
- Verification badges (ID Verified, Right to Work Checked, Admin Approved, DBS Verified if applicable)
- Reviews and star rating from other families
- Availability calendar

Take your time browsing profiles. Read their bios, look at their reviews, and find someone who feels like the right match.

---

### Step 3: Request a Booking

#### Choosing Your Caregiver
When you find a caregiver you'd like to work with, click "Request Booking" on their profile.

#### Booking Details
You'll specify:

**Date and Time**: Choose from the caregiver's available slots

**Duration**: Companionship sessions have a minimum booking of 2 hours (this covers travel time and ensures quality care time)

**Service Type**: Confirm what support you need (companionship visit, light housework, shopping assistance, meal preparation, or a combination)

**Special Requests**: Add any specific information the caregiver should know (e.g., "I'd love to chat about gardening" or "Help needed preparing a simple lunch")

**Emergency Contact**: Confirm your emergency contact details are up to date

#### Price Calculation
You'll see the total cost before requesting:
- Caregiver's hourly rate × number of hours = Total amount
- No hidden fees for families
- Payment is authorized but not charged until the caregiver accepts

#### Caregiver Response Window
Caregivers have 24 hours to accept or decline your booking request. They may decline if their schedule has changed or if they feel they're not the right match. If declined, you can request another caregiver - there's no penalty or fee.

---

### Step 4: Booking Confirmation

#### What Happens When a Caregiver Accepts

**Payment Captured**: Your card is charged and the payment is held securely in escrow (it's not released to the caregiver yet)

**Contact Details Shared**: You'll receive the caregiver's phone number and they'll receive yours so you can coordinate the visit

**Calendar Blocked**: The time is reserved in both your calendars

**Confirmation Email**: Both parties receive booking confirmation with all the details

#### Before the Visit
We recommend having a brief phone call or message exchange before the first visit to:
- Confirm exact arrival time
- Share any access instructions (door codes, parking)
- Discuss any last-minute details

---

### Step 5: The Companionship Visit

#### What to Expect
Your caregiver will arrive at the agreed time and provide the companionship and support you've arranged. This might include:

- Friendly conversation and company
- Shared activities (tea, cards, watching TV together, gentle walks)
- Light housework if requested
- Accompanied shopping trips
- Meal preparation together or for you

#### What's NOT Included (At This Stage)
Our current service scope is companionship only. We do **not** yet offer:
- Personal care (washing, dressing, toileting)
- Medical care or clinical support
- Medication administration
- Intensive mobility assistance requiring lifting

**These services are coming soon in our next phase** when we launch personal care support with enhanced verification.

#### During the Visit
- The caregiver is there to provide companionship and the specific support you've requested
- You (or your family member) remain in control - this is your home and your time
- If you have any concerns during the visit, you can contact us through the app

#### Emergency Guidance
**For medical emergencies, always call 999 first.** iCare is not an emergency service. Our platform is for arranged companionship care, not emergency medical response.

---

### Step 6: Booking Completion and Payment

#### Marking the Booking as Complete
After the visit, the caregiver marks the booking as complete in the app. This triggers the payment release.

#### Payment Release
The payment held in escrow is released to the caregiver within 2 business days. This escrow system protects both parties - you don't pay until care is provided, and caregivers are guaranteed payment once they complete the booking.

#### If There's a Problem
If the caregiver doesn't show up or doesn't complete the agreed care, contact our support team immediately. We'll investigate and can issue full refunds where appropriate.

---

### Step 7: Leave a Review

#### Why Reviews Matter
After your booking is complete, you'll be invited to leave a review. Your honest feedback helps other families make informed choices and helps caregivers build their reputation.

#### What to Include
- How did the caregiver make you (or your loved one) feel?
- Were they punctual and professional?
- Did they provide the companionship and support you expected?
- Would you book them again?

#### Review Moderation
All reviews are moderated by our admin team to ensure they're respectful, honest and helpful. We don't allow personal attacks, but we do encourage honest feedback - both positive and constructive.

**Light Housework**: Help with tidying, light cleaning, laundry - keeping your space comfortable without the strain.

**These services are coming soon** when we launch Tier 2 with enhanced verification including mandatory DBS checks, qualification verification, and insurance requirements.

## Verification Explained

### What "ID Verified" Means
Every caregiver completes government identity verification using Stripe Identity (or equivalent secure technology). This process:
- Confirms their identity document is genuine
- Checks their photo matches the document
- Verifies their name and date of birth
- Cannot be bypassed or faked

No caregiver can offer services without completing this check.

---

### What "Right to Work Verified" Means
Every caregiver proves they have legal right to work in the UK by providing:
- A UKVI share code (for non-UK nationals), or
- Document verification (UK passport or birth certificate)

We manually check every submission. No caregiver can work on the platform without legal right to work in the UK.

---

### What "Admin Approved" Means
After all verification checks are complete, every caregiver profile is manually reviewed by our admin team before it goes live. We check:
- Profile content is appropriate and professional
- All verification documents are valid
- Bio and services offered are clear and within scope
- No red flags or concerns

This is not an automated process. A real person reviews every caregiver.

---

### What "DBS Verified" Means (Optional at This Stage)

**Important Context**: Companionship services (conversation, light housework, shopping, meal prep) are **not legally classified as "regulated activity"** under the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006. This means DBS checks are not legally required for companionship-only caregivers.

**However**: Many caregivers already have DBS certificates from other care work. Caregivers can voluntarily upload their existing DBS certificate for verification. Our admin team checks:
- The certificate is genuine and current
- The certificate level is appropriate (Enhanced DBS preferred)
- The issue date is recent (ideally within last 3 years)

If verified, the caregiver receives a "DBS Verified" badge on their profile. You can filter your search to show only DBS Verified caregivers if this is important to you.

**When Personal Care Launches**: DBS checks will become **mandatory** for all caregivers offering personal care services (washing, dressing, toileting). We're being transparent about our progressive approach to building trust.

## Safety and Safeguarding

### Our Safeguarding Commitment
iCare complies with the **Care Act 2014** safeguarding duties. We have:

- A designated safeguarding lead
- Clear safeguarding policies and procedures
- 24/7 incident reporting mechanisms
- Relationships with local Safeguarding Adults Boards (SABs)
- Audit trails of all platform actions

---

### How to Report a Concern
If you have any safeguarding concerns about a caregiver, a booking, or a situation you've witnessed:

**In the App**: Use the "Report Concern" button available on every caregiver profile and booking

**By Email**: safeguarding@icare-app.co.uk (monitored 24/7)

**By Phone**: [NUMBER] (24-hour safeguarding line)

All concerns are reviewed within 24 hours for non-urgent issues, immediately for urgent concerns.

---

### What Happens When You Report
1. **Acknowledgment**: We acknowledge your report within 24 hours
2. **Investigation**: Our safeguarding team reviews the concern and may contact you for more information
3. **Action**: We may suspend accounts, investigate further, or escalate to local authorities
4. **Outcome**: You'll be informed of the outcome where appropriate (some details may be confidential)

---

### Emergency Contact System
When you create your profile, you provide emergency contact information. This is stored securely and:
- Shared with the caregiver before each visit (so they know who to contact in an emergency)
- Available to our admin team if needed
- Can be updated at any time

### Introduction Agency Model

**What This Means For You**:

iCare is an **Introduction Agency**, not a care provider. This means:

- We connect you with **self-employed professional caregivers**
- The caregivers are **not employees** of iCare
- You choose which caregiver to engage
- The caregiver is professionally responsible for the care they provide
- We provide ID verification, payment processing, and safeguarding reporting infrastructure

**Platform Responsibilities**:
- Verify caregiver identity and right to work
- Process payments securely
- Provide safeguarding reporting mechanisms
- Admin oversight and incident management

**Your Responsibilities**:
- Choose caregivers appropriate for your needs
- Provide accurate information
- Report concerns if they arise
- Work with the caregiver to coordinate care

**Caregiver Responsibilities**:
- Provide professional, safe companionship care
- Arrive on time and fulfill the booking
- Report any concerns to iCare or emergency services
- Maintain professional conduct

## Pricing

### How Pricing Works
Caregivers set their own hourly rates (typical range: 12-25 GBP per hour depending on experience, location, and services offered).

**What You Pay**:
- The caregiver's hourly rate × number of hours booked
- **No additional fees for families**
- **No hidden charges**

Platform commission is paid by the caregiver, not by you.

See our [Pricing Page](/pricing) for detailed examples and FAQs.

---

### Cancellation Policy

**More than 24 hours before booking**: Full refund, no questions asked

**Less than 24 hours before booking**: 50% refund (partial compensation to caregiver for short-notice cancellation)

**No-show** (you're not home when caregiver arrives): No refund (caregiver has traveled and honored their commitment)

We understand circumstances change. We try to be fair to both families and caregivers in our cancellation policy.

---

### Payment Protection
All payments are:
- Processed through Stripe (industry-leading secure payment platform)
- Held in escrow until care is completed
- Never shared off-platform
- Protected by our Terms of Service

**We prohibit off-platform payments**. If a caregiver asks you to pay them directly (cash, bank transfer), report this immediately. Off-platform payments bypass our safeguarding and payment protection.
