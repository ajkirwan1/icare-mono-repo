## Emergency Guidance

### Section Headline
**In an Emergency**

### Medical Emergency or Immediate Danger

**Call 999 First**

iCare is **not an emergency service**. If you or your loved one are experiencing a medical emergency or are in immediate danger:

1. **Call 999** (police, ambulance, fire)
2. Ensure immediate safety
3. **Then** notify iCare safeguarding team: safeguarding@icare.co.uk or [SAFEGUARDING HOTLINE]

We can coordinate with emergency services, suspend accounts if needed, and document the incident, but we cannot provide emergency response.

---

### Urgent Safeguarding Concerns

If someone is at risk of harm but not in immediate danger:

**Contact Our Safeguarding Team**:
- Email: safeguarding@icare.co.uk (checked 24/7)
- Phone: [SAFEGUARDING HOTLINE]

**Local Safeguarding Adults Board**:
If you prefer to report directly to authorities, contact your local Safeguarding Adults Board (SAB). Find your local SAB: [https://www.gov.uk/report-abuse-of-older-person](https://www.gov.uk/report-abuse-of-older-person)

## Response Time Expectations

### What to Expect When You Contact Us

**Email Inquiries**: 24-48 hours (usually same or next day)

**Phone Calls**: Answered during business hours, voicemail returned next business day

**Safeguarding Concerns**: Urgent within hours, non-urgent within 24 hours

**Formal Complaints**: Acknowledged within 48 hours, resolved within 7 days

**Caregiver Verification**: 3-5 business days for profile approval

**We're a Small Team**: We read every message personally and respond thoughtfully. During busy periods or holidays, responses may take slightly longer, but we'll always get back to you.

## What to Include When Contacting Us

### Help Us Help You Faster

**For Support Issues**:
- Your account email address
- Booking reference number (if applicable)
- Screenshot of error message (if technical issue)
- What you were trying to do when the problem occurred
- What device/browser you're using

**For Safeguarding Concerns**:
- Names of people involved
- Booking details (date, time, reference)
- Description of concern
- Any evidence (only if safe to provide)
- Your contact details (unless reporting anonymously)

**For Caregiver Applications**:
- Email address used to apply
- Date of application
- Specific verification question

The more information you provide, the faster we can help.
