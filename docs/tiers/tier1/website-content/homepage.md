## Trust Signals Section

### Section Headline
**Your Safety is Our Priority**

### Section Subheadline
Every caregiver on iCare goes through verification before they can offer services. We're building trust step by step.

### Trust Point Cards

#### ID Verified
Every caregiver completes government ID verification using secure identity checking technology. We confirm their identity, check their photo matches, and verify document authenticity.

**Badge**: "ID Verified" checkmark

---

#### Right to Work Verified
All caregivers provide proof of their legal right to work in the UK through UKVI share codes or document verification. We ensure every caregiver is legally permitted to provide care services.

**Badge**: "Right to Work Checked"

---

#### Admin Approved Profiles
Before any caregiver profile goes live, our admin team manually reviews their profile, checks their verification documents, and ensures they meet our standards. No automated approvals - every caregiver is reviewed by a real person.

**Badge**: "Admin Approved"

---

#### Voluntary DBS Option
While DBS checks are not legally required for companionship services, caregivers who have existing DBS certificates can upload them for verification. Look for the "DBS Verified" badge when searching to identify caregivers who have voluntarily provided this additional check.

**Badge**: "DBS Verified" (optional badge shown on relevant profiles)

---

#### Secure Payments
All payments are processed through Stripe, held securely in escrow until care is completed, then released to the caregiver. Your card details are never shared with caregivers, and all transactions are protected.

**Badge**: "Secure Payments" or Stripe logo

---

#### 24/7 Safeguarding Support
Report concerns at any time through our in-app reporting system. We comply with the Care Act 2014 safeguarding duties and work with local Safeguarding Adults Boards when needed. Your safety and the safety of your loved ones is our absolute priority.

**Badge**: "Care Act Compliant"

---

#### Reviews and Ratings
Every completed booking can be reviewed. Read honest feedback from other families to help you choose the right caregiver. Our admin team moderates all reviews to ensure they're helpful, respectful and genuine.

**Badge**: Star rating icon

## Introduction Agency Notice Section

### Section Headline
**How We Work: Introduction Agency Model**

### Section Copy
iCare is an Introduction Agency, not a care provider. This means:

- We connect you with **self-employed professional caregivers**
- Caregivers are **not employees** of iCare - they are independent professionals
- We provide ID verification, payment processing, and safeguarding support
- You choose which caregiver to engage
- The caregiver is professionally responsible for the care they provide

This model gives caregivers flexibility and control while giving families choice and transparency.

## Compliance Notes

### Required Disclaimers
1. **Not a Care Provider**: "iCare is an Introduction Agency connecting self-employed caregivers with families. We do not provide care directly."

2. **Emergency Guidance**: "For medical emergencies, always call 999. iCare is not an emergency service."

3. **Service Scope**: "Currently offering companionship services only. Personal care services coming soon."

4. **DBS Transparency**: "DBS checks are voluntary at this stage as companionship is not legally classified as a regulated activity. DBS becomes mandatory when we launch personal care services."

### Accessibility Considerations
- All content written at reading age 12-14 level
- Clear headings and logical page structure
- Alt text for all images
- High contrast color scheme
- Large, readable fonts
- Mobile-first responsive design

### Design Team Notes
1. **Imagery**: All photographs should show real caregivers and care receivers (or models) in authentic situations. Avoid stock photos that look staged. Represent diverse ethnicities, ages, and abilities.

2. **Tone**: Warm but professional. Reassuring without being patronizing. Clear without being clinical.

3. **Trust Signals**: Make verification badges prominent but not overwhelming. Trust should feel earned, not marketed.

4. **CTAs**: Primary CTAs should be high-contrast and clearly labeled. Secondary CTAs softer but still visible.

5. **White Space**: Don't overcrowd. This is for elderly users and their families - clarity over density.
