## General Questions {#general}

### What is iCare?

iCare is a UK-based online platform connecting elderly adults and their families with verified professional caregivers for companionship services.

At this stage (Tier 1), we offer companionship, light housework, shopping, and meal preparation. Personal care services (washing, dressing, toileting) are coming soon in Tier 2.

---

### What services are available at launch?

**Currently Available** (Tier 1):
- Companionship visits (conversation, shared activities)
- Light housework and tidying
- Shopping and errands
- Meal preparation (no feeding assistance)
- Transportation (if caregiver has vehicle)

**NOT Yet Available**:
- Personal care (washing, dressing, toileting)
- Live-in care
- Condition-specific matching
- Medical or clinical services

See our [roadmap](/about#roadmap) for when additional services will launch.

---

### What is an Introduction Agency?

An Introduction Agency connects families with self-employed professional caregivers but does not provide care directly.

**What this means**:
- iCare introduces you to caregivers
- Caregivers are self-employed, not iCare employees
- You choose which caregiver to engage
- The caregiver is professionally responsible for care they provide
- iCare provides verification, payment processing, and safeguarding infrastructure

This is different from a care agency that employs caregivers and provides care directly.

---

### When will personal care services be available?

We're targeting **6-12 months** after our Tier 1 launch, depending on achieving success metrics (active users, completed bookings, safety performance).

When personal care launches:
- DBS checks become mandatory (vs. voluntary now)
- Qualification verification required
- Insurance verification required
- Enhanced safeguarding measures

**Want early access?** Join our waitlist by creating a profile now.

---

### Is iCare CQC-registered?

No. iCare is an Introduction Agency, not a care provider, so CQC registration is not required.

CQC regulates organizations that provide care services directly. We connect families with self-employed caregivers, but we don't provide care ourselves.

We do, however, comply with **Care Act 2014 safeguarding duties** which apply regardless of CQC status.

---

### What areas do you cover?

**UK-wide**. Caregivers across England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland can join, and families can search for caregivers anywhere in the UK.

Availability depends on caregiver supply in your area. Urban areas typically have more caregivers than rural areas.

---

### How is iCare different from other care platforms?

**Our Differences**:
- **Progressive Trust**: We're honest about starting with companionship and building toward personal care with enhanced verification
- **Safety First**: Every decision filtered through "Does this protect vulnerable adults?"
- **Fair to Caregivers**: They set rates, control schedules, keep 90% of earnings
- **Transparent Pricing**: No hidden fees, clear commission structure
- **UK-Focused**: Built for UK regulations, UK care culture, UK users

## For Families and Care Receivers {#families}

### How are caregivers verified?

**Mandatory Verification (All Caregivers)**:
- ✅ Government ID verification (Stripe Identity)
- ✅ Right to work verification (UKVI share code or documents)
- ✅ Phone verification (SMS code)
- ✅ Email verification
- ✅ Manual admin approval (human review of every profile)

**Voluntary Verification**:
- ⭐ DBS certificate upload (earns "DBS Verified" badge)

See [Trust & Safety](/trust-and-safety) for full details.

---

### Is DBS required for companionship services?

No, DBS checks are not legally required for companionship services because companionship is not classified as "regulated activity" under the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006.

**However**:
- Caregivers with existing DBS certificates can upload for verification
- They receive a "DBS Verified" badge
- You can filter search to show only DBS Verified caregivers

**When personal care launches**, DBS will become **mandatory** for all caregivers offering those services.

---

### How much does it cost?

**For Families**:
- You pay the caregiver's hourly rate × number of hours
- No additional fees for families
- Typical rates: 12-25 GBP/hour depending on caregiver experience and location
- 2-hour minimum booking

**Example**:
- Caregiver charges 18 GBP/hour
- 3-hour booking = 54 GBP
- That's what you pay (no hidden fees)

See [Pricing](/pricing) for detailed examples.

---

### How do I pay?

All payments through the platform via **Stripe** (credit/debit cards).

**Payment Flow**:
1. Request booking → card authorized (not charged yet)
2. Caregiver accepts → payment captured and held in escrow
3. Care completed → payment released to caregiver (2 business days)

You never pay cash or directly to caregivers. On-platform payments only.

---

### Can I choose my own caregiver?

Yes. You browse profiles, read reviews, see ratings, and decide who to request a booking with.

The choice is always yours.

---

### What if I need to cancel?

**More than 24 hours before booking**: Full refund

**Less than 24 hours before booking**: 50% refund (partial compensation to caregiver)

**No-show** (you're not home): No refund (caregiver traveled and honored commitment)

**Caregiver no-show**: Full refund + caregiver may face suspension

---

### What if I'm not happy with the care provided?

Contact our support team immediately: support@icare.co.uk

We'll investigate and can facilitate partial or full refunds where appropriate. We take complaints seriously and will address issues with caregivers.

---

### What if I need personal care now?

We're not yet offering personal care services (washing, dressing, toileting). We recommend contacting a CQC-registered domiciliary care agency for immediate personal care needs.

You can create a profile on iCare now to access personal care when we launch it (estimated 6-12 months).

---

### Can a family member book on behalf of someone else?

Yes. You can register as a family member and create a profile/book care on behalf of an elderly relative.

Many families coordinate care together - we understand and support this.

---

### What happens in an emergency?

**Medical Emergency**: Call 999 first. iCare is not an emergency service.

**Safeguarding Concern**: Report via in-app button, email safeguarding@icare.co.uk, or call [SAFEGUARDING HOTLINE].

---

### Do I need to be 65+ to use iCare?

No strict age requirement, but iCare is designed for elderly adults or adults with care needs. If you're under 65 but have documented care needs, you're welcome to use the platform.

---

### Can I request the same caregiver regularly?

Yes. If you find a caregiver you like, you can book them repeatedly. Build a relationship with a caregiver you trust.

Many families develop regular schedules with preferred caregivers.

## For Caregivers {#caregivers}

### Do I need a DBS check to join?

No, DBS is **voluntary** at Tier 1 (companionship services).

**If you have an existing DBS certificate**, upload it for verification to earn a "DBS Verified" badge. Many families prefer DBS-verified caregivers.

**When personal care launches** (Tier 2), DBS will become **mandatory** for all personal care caregivers.

---

### How do I get paid?

Automatically, within **2 business days** of completing a booking.

**Process**:
1. Family books and pays (held in escrow)
2. You provide care
3. Mark booking as complete
4. Payment released to your bank account (2 business days)

No invoicing, no chasing payments. Completely automatic.

---

### What commission do you charge?

**10%** of each completed booking.

**Example**:
- You charge 20 GBP/hour
- 3-hour booking = 60 GBP
- Commission (10%) = 6 GBP
- You receive = 54 GBP

See [Pricing](/pricing) for more examples.

---

### Can I set my own rates?

Yes. You decide your hourly rate based on your experience, qualifications, and local market.

Typical range: 12-25 GBP/hour.

You can change your rate anytime, but existing booking requests honor the rate advertised when requested.

---

### What if I already work for an agency?

No problem. You're self-employed on iCare, so you can work for multiple platforms or agencies simultaneously.

Many caregivers use iCare to fill gaps in their schedule.

---

### What services can I offer?

**Tier 1 (Currently)**:
- Companionship visits
- Light housework
- Shopping and errands
- Meal preparation
- Transportation (if you have a vehicle)

**Coming Soon (Tier 2)**:
- Personal care (washing, dressing, toileting)
- Mobility assistance
- Medication prompting
- Overnight care

---

### How do I get more bookings?

**Build Your Profile**:
- Professional photo
- Warm, detailed bio
- Clear service descriptions
- Competitive rates for your area
- DBS Verified badge (if you upload certificate)

**Build Your Reputation**:
- Complete bookings professionally
- Earn 5-star reviews
- Build rating over time

**Stay Active**:
- Keep availability calendar updated
- Respond to booking requests within 24 hours
- Accept bookings you can fulfill

---

### What are my tax responsibilities?

You're self-employed, so you must:
- Register with HMRC for Self Assessment
- Declare all iCare income
- Pay income tax on earnings
- Pay Class 2 and Class 4 National Insurance
- File annual tax return by January 31st

**iCare does NOT**:
- Deduct tax from your payments
- Pay employer National Insurance
- File tax returns on your behalf

Consult an accountant for tax advice.

**Resources**:
- HMRC Self Assessment: gov.uk/self-assessment-tax-returns
- HMRC Helpline: 0300 200 3310

---

### Can I decline booking requests?

Yes, with **no penalty**.

You're self-employed - you choose which bookings to accept. Reasons for declining:
- Schedule conflict
- Location too far
- Outside your comfort zone
- Already accepted another booking

No acceptance quota or minimum requirements.

---

### How long does verification take?

Typically **3-5 days**:
- ID verification: Instant to 24 hours
- Right to work: 1-2 business days
- DBS (if uploaded): 2-3 business days
- Admin profile review: 1-2 business days

Once approved, your profile goes live and you can start receiving booking requests.

---

### Can I pause my profile if I need a break?

Yes. You can set yourself as unavailable in the calendar, or contact support to temporarily deactivate your profile.

No penalties for taking time off. Reactivate whenever you're ready.

---

### Do I need insurance?

**At Tier 1**: Insurance is recommended but not required.

We recommend **Public Liability Insurance** (covers accidental damage or injury while providing care).

**At Tier 2** (personal care): Professional Liability Insurance will become **mandatory**.

## Safety and Safeguarding {#safety}

### How do you protect vulnerable adults?

**Multiple Layers**:
- ID verification for all caregivers
- Right to work verification
- Manual admin approval
- Voluntary DBS option
- 24/7 safeguarding reporting
- Admin oversight and investigation
- Cooperation with Safeguarding Adults Boards (SABs)
- Care Act 2014 compliance
- Reviews and ratings system
- Escrow payment protection

See [Trust & Safety](/trust-and-safety) for complete details.

---

### What if there's an emergency during a visit?

**Medical Emergency**: Call 999 first. Then notify iCare.

iCare is not an emergency response service. Emergency services should always be your first call.

After ensuring safety, report to iCare so we can document and take platform-level action if needed.

---

### How do I report a safeguarding concern?

**In the App**: "Report Concern" button on profiles and bookings

**Email**: safeguarding@icare.co.uk (monitored 24/7)

**Phone**: [SAFEGUARDING HOTLINE] (24-hour response)

All reports taken seriously. Response within 24 hours for non-urgent, immediate for urgent concerns.

---

### What safeguarding policies are in place?

We comply with **Care Act 2014** safeguarding duties:
- Designated safeguarding lead
- 24/7 incident reporting mechanisms
- Admin investigation procedures
- Escalation to Safeguarding Adults Boards when appropriate
- Cooperation with police and emergency services
- Audit trails of all actions
- User suspension/ban powers
- Documented safeguarding policy

---

### What should I do if I witness abuse or neglect?

**Report immediately**:

**In Immediate Danger**: Call 999 first

**Then Report to iCare**:
- In-app "Report Concern"
- Email: safeguarding@icare.co.uk
- Phone: [SAFEGUARDING HOTLINE]

We investigate all reports and escalate to appropriate authorities (police, SABs, social services) when needed.

---

### Can caregivers see my address before I book?

No. Caregivers see your general area (postcode district) and distance from them, but not your exact address.

Your full address is only shared once a caregiver accepts your booking.

---

### What if a caregiver asks me to pay cash?

🚨 **Report immediately** - this is a serious violation.

All payments must go through the platform. Off-platform payments (cash, bank transfer) bypass all protections and are prohibited.

Report via:
- In-app "Report Concern"
- Email: safeguarding@icare.co.uk

Caregiver may face suspension or ban.

## Pricing and Payments {#pricing}

### Are there any hidden fees?

**For Families**: No. You pay the caregiver's hourly rate × hours. No booking fees, no service charges.

**For Caregivers**: No. Just 10% commission per booking. No monthly fees, no listing fees, no payout fees.

---

### Why do caregivers pay commission instead of families?

This model is common in professional service marketplaces. It keeps pricing transparent for families (what you see is what you pay) while ensuring caregivers who benefit from platform infrastructure contribute to costs.

---

### Can I pay the caregiver directly?

No. All payments must go through the platform.

**Why**:
- Escrow protection (you don't pay until care is provided)
- Refund ability if there's a problem
- Safeguarding: track transactions for exploitation concerns
- Fair: caregivers can't bypass the verification platform

Off-platform payments are prohibited and should be reported.

---

### What if I need to cancel?

**More than 24 hours before**: Full refund
**Less than 24 hours**: 50% refund
**No-show**: No refund

See full [Cancellation Policy](/pricing#cancellation-policy).

---

### When am I charged?

**Booking Request**: Card authorized (not charged yet)
**Caregiver Accepts**: Payment captured and held in escrow
**Care Completed**: Payment released to caregiver (2 business days)

---

### What payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Stripe.

We don't accept cash, cheques, or direct bank transfers.

---

### Is my payment information secure?

Yes. All payments processed through **Stripe** with bank-level encryption and PCI DSS compliance.

Your card details are never shared with caregivers or stored on our servers.

---

### Can I get a receipt for tax purposes?

Yes. All booking confirmations include payment receipts. Download payment history from your account dashboard.

---

### How much do caregivers actually receive?

Caregivers receive **90%** of their advertised rate (after 10% platform commission).

**Example**:
- Caregiver rate: 20 GBP/hour
- 3-hour booking: 60 GBP (what family pays)
- Commission: 6 GBP (10%)
- Caregiver receives: 54 GBP (90%)

---

### When do caregivers get paid?

Within **2 business days** of marking the booking as complete.

Payments are automatic - no invoicing required.

---

### Do caregivers pay tax?

Yes. Caregivers are self-employed and responsible for their own income tax and National Insurance.

iCare does not deduct tax from payments.

---

### Can families tip caregivers?

Not through the platform at this stage.

If a family wants to show appreciation:
- Leave a glowing 5-star review
- Book the caregiver again regularly
- Recommend to friends and family

Reviews and repeat bookings are the best "tips" for building a caregiver's reputation and income.

---

### How do caregivers set their hourly rate?

Caregivers choose their own rate during profile setup based on:
- Experience and qualifications
- Local market rates
- Services offered
- Availability

They can change rates anytime in their profile settings.

---

### Can caregivers negotiate rates with families?

No. Advertised rates are fixed to keep pricing transparent and avoid awkward negotiations.

If a caregiver's rate isn't attracting bookings, they can adjust it in their settings.

---

### What if there's a payment dispute?

Contact support immediately: support@icare.co.uk

We investigate all payment disputes and ensure fair outcomes. We can issue refunds to families or ensure caregivers receive payment as appropriate.

- Encryption at rest and in transit
- Secure authentication
- Regular security audits
- Access controls

### What data do you collect?
