## 1. Introduction

If you're a caregiver, you already know what's broken. You've felt the frustration of 15-minute visits where you barely have time to say hello before rushing to the next person. You've experienced the exhaustion of schedules that change without warning, travel time that goes unpaid, and the hollow feeling of being treated like a number instead of a professional.

You didn't get into care work for this. You got into it because you wanted to help people. You wanted to make someone's day brighter. You wanted meaningful work where you could use your compassion, your patience, and your genuine care for others. You wanted to build relationships, not tick boxes.

If that sounds familiar, we're building iCare for you.

This page explains what we're creating, how you'll be able to build your own practice as a companion caregiver, and why we believe there's a better way to do this work. It's about respect, flexibility, and putting the people who actually provide care at the heart of how this platform works.

---

## 2. What We're Creating

We're building a platform where caregivers who value quality over quantity can connect directly with families seeking companionship for elderly relatives. This isn't another agency that controls your schedule and takes most of your earnings. It isn't a job board that leaves you to figure everything out alone. It's something different.

Think of iCare as a space where you can build your own practice. You'll create a profile that shows who you are and what you offer. Families in your area will be able to find you. You'll have the freedom to set your own availability, choose who you work with, and focus on providing the kind of care you actually believe in. The kind where you can sit down for a proper conversation. Where you can take someone for a walk without watching the clock. Where you're valued for being present, not just for completing tasks.

We're starting with companionship services because that's where connection matters most. Companionship is about spending quality time with someone. It's having a cup of tea and a chat. It's going to the park together. It's helping with a jigsaw puzzle or watching a favourite programme. It's the warmth of genuine human company. This is meaningful work, and the people who do it well deserve to be respected as professionals.

We believe caregivers should have flexibility and control over their working lives. We believe you should be able to build real relationships with the people you support, not rush through standardised visits. We believe the care sector needs to change, and that change starts with treating caregivers with the dignity and fairness you deserve.

Right now, we're building. We're creating something better from the ground up, and we want caregivers who share these values to be part of it from the beginning.

## 3. How You'll Build Your Practice

When iCare launches, you'll be able to create your own practice as a companion caregiver. Here's the journey you'll take.

### Step 1: Create Your Profile

You'll start by creating a profile that shows who you are. Not just a list of qualifications or tick-box skills, but the real you. What kind of companionship do you enjoy providing? What are your interests and hobbies that you could share with someone? What makes you the kind of person families would trust to spend time with their loved ones?

Your profile will be your introduction to families. It's where they'll get a sense of whether you're the right fit for their parent, relative, or spouse. You'll be able to share what matters to you about care work and what you bring to the relationships you build.

### Step 2: Set Your Own Terms

You'll have control over your availability and your schedule. Want to work mornings only? Prefer weekends? Looking for regular commitments or flexible one-off visits? You decide. This is your practice, and you set the terms that work for your life.

You'll be able to choose the areas you're willing to travel to and the types of companionship you want to provide. If you love taking people out for walks, you can focus on that. If you prefer quiet indoor company, that's fine too. You're building something that works for you, not fitting into someone else's system.

### Step 3: Connect with Families

Families seeking companionship will be able to find your profile and reach out to you directly. You'll see their requests and decide which ones feel like the right fit. You'll have the opportunity to learn about what they're looking for and make an informed choice about whether it's something you want to take on.

This isn't about accepting every job that comes your way. It's about building relationships with people you genuinely want to support. You'll have the ability to communicate with families, ask questions, and make sure you're a good match before committing to anything.

### Step 4: Provide Meaningful Care

When you accept a companionship arrangement, you'll be able to do what you do best: be present with someone who needs company. You'll have the time to build a real relationship. You'll get to know their stories, their interests, their sense of humour. You'll become someone they look forward to seeing, not just another face in a parade of rushed carers.

This is the work that drew you to care in the first place. The work where you make a genuine difference in someone's life. The work that feels meaningful because you're treating people with dignity and giving them your full attention.

### Step 5: Grow Your Reputation

Over time, as you build relationships and provide quality care, you'll develop a reputation on the platform. Families will be able to leave reviews and feedback. Your profile will show that you're reliable, trustworthy, and good at what you do. This reputation will help you attract more families who are looking for exactly what you offer.

You'll be building something that's yours. A practice based on your values, your approach to care, and the relationships you've created. That's what we want to enable.

## 4. What Makes This Different

If you've worked in traditional care, you know what's wrong with the system. We're building iCare to be different in the ways that actually matter to caregivers.

**Flexibility and control.** You set your own schedule. You choose who you work with. You decide what kind of companionship you want to provide. This isn't an agency telling you where to be and when. This is your practice, and you're in control.

**Respect for your professionalism.** Caregiving is skilled, meaningful work. It requires patience, empathy, emotional intelligence, and the ability to connect with people in vulnerable situations. We treat it that way. You're not a number on a rota. You're a professional providing an important service.

**Real relationships, not transactions.** You'll have time to actually get to know the people you support. You can build the kind of connections that make this work rewarding. No more 15-minute visits where you barely have time to catch your breath. This is about quality, not quantity.

**A community of like-minded caregivers.** You'll be part of a community of people who share your values. People who believe care should be done well, with dignity and respect. People who want meaningful work, not just a paycheque. We're building this together.

**Fairness and transparency.** We're designing this platform to be fair to the people who actually do the work. We're not here to extract as much value as possible from caregivers. We're here to create a sustainable model where everyone benefits. More details will be available when we launch, but our values are clear: caregivers deserve to be treated fairly.

This is what's different about iCare. It's built around what caregivers actually need, not what's easiest for an agency to manage.

## 5. Who We're Looking For

We're looking for caregivers who believe in quality over quantity. People who understand that companionship isn't just about being in the same room as someone—it's about genuine connection and presence.

You don't need formal qualifications to provide companionship care. What matters most is who you are as a person. Are you patient and kind? Can you listen well and make people feel heard? Do you treat elderly adults with dignity and respect? Are you reliable and trustworthy? Those are the qualities that make a great companion caregiver.

Maybe you've worked in care for years and you're looking for a better way to use your experience. Maybe you've cared for your own family members and discovered you have a gift for connection. Maybe you're exploring care work for the first time and want to start in a way that feels meaningful. Whatever your background, if you care about doing this work well, we want to hear from you.

We're looking for people who are compassionate, reliable, and respectful. People who understand that the elderly adults they support deserve to be treated as individuals, not tasks. People who want to build relationships and make a real difference in someone's life.

If that sounds like you, we'd love you to join our founding community.
