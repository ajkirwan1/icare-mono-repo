## Page Content

### Introduction

**H1: Our Commitment to Safety**

When you're inviting someone into your loved one's life - or when you're entering someone's home as a caregiver - trust isn't optional. It's everything.

That's why safety isn't a feature we're adding to iCare. It's the foundation of everything we're building. We know that without trust, nothing else matters. And we know that trust must be earned through actions, not just words.

This page explains our approach to safety - the principles that guide every decision we make, and the commitments we're making to families and caregivers alike.

---

### Our Safety Principles

**H2: Our Safety Principles**

These five principles shape how we're building iCare. They're not marketing messages - they're the standards we hold ourselves to.

#### 1. Identity First

**We verify who people are.**

You deserve to know who you're working with. We're building thorough verification into our platform from day one. Before anyone can connect through iCare, we take steps to confirm their identity and ensure they're who they say they are.

This isn't a checkbox exercise. It's a fundamental requirement that protects everyone in our community.

#### 2. Choice and Control

**You decide who you work with.**

Safety isn't just about verification - it's about empowerment. Families choose which caregivers they want to work with. Caregivers choose which families they want to support. No one is forced into a relationship they're not comfortable with.

You have full control over who enters your life or your loved one's life. That control is yours to keep.

#### 3. Transparency

**Clear information to make informed decisions.**

You can't make good decisions without good information. We're committed to giving you the details you need about who you're considering working with - presented clearly, honestly, and without hidden fine print.

Transparency builds trust. And trust is what makes good care possible.

#### 4. Support When Needed

**Help is available if something goes wrong.**

We hope you never need it, but if something doesn't feel right - if there's a concern, a problem, or even just a question - there will be someone to talk to. We're building support systems to respond when you need help.

Safety isn't just prevention. It's also knowing that help is there when you need it.

#### 5. Continuous Improvement

**We're always getting safer.**

We won't get everything right on day one. But we're committed to learning, improving, and responding when we discover better ways to protect our community.

Safety is a journey, not a destination. We'll listen, adapt, and always prioritise doing right by the people who trust us.

### For Families

**H2: For Families: Peace of Mind Starts Here**

We understand what you're worried about. Your loved one is vulnerable. Their safety, dignity, and wellbeing matter more than anything. Inviting a stranger into their life - into their home - is a big decision.

Here's what we want you to know.

**We take this as seriously as you do.** Your parent, spouse, or relative matters to us too. Not as a transaction or a user profile, but as a real person deserving of respect, care, and protection.

**We're building safety into every step.** From the moment someone joins our platform to the moment they connect with your loved one, we're thinking about risk, trust, and safeguarding. Not as an afterthought, but as the core of what we do.

**We'll give you the information you need.** You'll be able to see caregiver profiles, read about their experience, and understand who they are before making a decision. You won't be pressured into working with someone you don't trust.

**You're not alone in this.** If you have concerns, questions, or something doesn't feel right, we'll be here to listen and help. Your peace of mind matters.

We can't promise that care will always be perfect - no one can. But we can promise that we'll never take shortcuts when it comes to safety. Your loved one deserves that. So do you.

---

### For Caregivers

**H2: For Caregivers: We Protect You Too**

Safety isn't just about the people receiving care. It's about protecting caregivers too.

If you're joining iCare as a caregiver, here's what you can expect from us.

**A professional environment.** You deserve to work in an environment where boundaries are respected, where you feel safe entering someone's home, and where your wellbeing matters. We're building a platform that takes your safety seriously.

**Support when you need it.** If something feels wrong - if a family makes you uncomfortable, if a situation feels unsafe, or if you just need guidance - there will be someone to talk to. You shouldn't have to navigate difficult situations alone.

**A community built on trust.** The verification process we're building protects you as much as it protects families. It ensures that everyone in our community has been properly welcomed and that trust flows both ways.

**Clear expectations.** You'll know what's expected of you, what families are looking for, and what kind of work you're agreeing to. No surprises. No hidden terms. Just honest, clear communication.

We know that caregiving can sometimes put you in vulnerable situations. We're committed to building a platform that respects your professionalism, protects your safety, and supports you when challenges arise.

---

### Building Trust Together

**H2: Building Trust Together**

Here's the truth: we can't create a safe platform on our own. Safety is something we build together - families, caregivers, and the iCare team.

**We're taking a progressive approach.** We're not launching with every feature on day one. We're starting with companionship care - meaningful, important work that helps tackle loneliness and brings connection to elderly adults. As we learn, grow, and prove ourselves, we'll expand carefully and thoughtfully.

**We'll be transparent about our journey.** We won't pretend to have all the answers. When we face challenges, we'll be honest. When we make improvements, we'll share them. When we don't know something, we'll say so.

**We need your help.** If you see something that concerns you, if you have an idea that could make us safer, or if you experience something that doesn't feel right - tell us. We're building this together, and your voice matters.

**Trust takes time.** We know that. We're not asking for blind faith. We're asking for the chance to prove ourselves through our actions, our choices, and our commitment to doing the right thing even when it's hard.

Trust in elderly care has been broken too many times by organisations that cut corners, prioritised profit over people, or made promises they didn't keep. We're building something different. And we need partners who believe that care can - and should - be better.

### Our Promise

**H2: Our Promise to You**

Safety will never be a marketing message at iCare. It will always be our foundation.

We promise to:
- Put people before profit when safety is at stake
- Build verification and safeguarding into everything we do
- Give you the information and control you need to make good decisions
- Be transparent about what we know and what we're still learning
- Support you when things go wrong
- Listen, improve, and never stop getting safer

We're building iCare because elderly care deserves better. Because families deserve peace of mind. Because caregivers deserve a professional, respectful environment. And because trust should be the foundation of every care relationship, not a luxury.

If that's the kind of platform you want to be part of - if you believe care can be safer, more transparent, and more trustworthy - we'd love to have you join us.

**Join the waitlist today.** Be part of building something better.

## Compliance Notes

### Required Legal Review
This page should be reviewed by legal counsel for:
- Claims about verification (we state we verify but don't detail methods)
- Safety commitments (we promise to take safety seriously but don't guarantee outcomes)
- Support availability (we say support will be available but don't specify response times)

### Regulatory Considerations
- **No CQC references**: Correctly omitted
- **No specific verification methods**: Correctly omitted (no mention of DBS, background checks, etc.)
- **No guarantees of safety**: Correctly framed as commitment and principle, not outcome guarantee
- **No regulatory positioning**: Correctly omitted (no Care Act, Introduction Agency, etc.)

### Claims That Require Evidence
The following claims should be supported with operational reality before launch:
- "Support is available if something goes wrong" - requires actual support system
- "We verify who people are" - requires implemented verification process
- "Help is there when you need it" - requires support channels
