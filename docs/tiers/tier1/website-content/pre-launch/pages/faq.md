## General Questions

### What is iCare?

iCare is a platform connecting families with trusted companions for elderly relatives across the UK. We're making it easier for families to find caring people who can spend quality time with their loved ones, and for caregivers to build meaningful, flexible practices. We're starting with companionship services because connection matters most.

### When will iCare launch?

We're working hard to launch in the coming months. We're building this thoughtfully to ensure we get the important things right, particularly around trust and safety. Families and caregivers on our waitlist will be the first to know when we're ready to launch and will get priority access to the platform.

### Where will iCare be available?

We're launching across the UK. We're building a national platform designed to serve families and caregivers throughout England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. As we grow, we'll work to ensure strong caregiver availability in communities across the country.

### How is iCare different from care agencies?

Traditional care agencies typically assign whoever is available on their rota without giving you real choice. They often feel rushed and transactional. iCare is fundamentally different. We give families genuine choice over who spends time with their loved one. We enable caregivers to build their own practices with flexibility and control. We focus on relationships, not transactions, and we build trust and safety into everything from the ground up.

### Is iCare a care agency?

No, we're not a traditional care agency. We're a platform that connects families directly with independent companion caregivers. We handle verification and safeguarding, but families choose who they work with and caregivers control their own schedules and practice. This model gives everyone more choice, flexibility, and control.

## For Families

### What services will be available?

We're launching with companionship services. Companionship is about spending quality time together - going for walks, having conversations over tea, helping with puzzles or hobbies, watching favourite programmes, accompanying someone to the shops, or simply sitting in comfortable company. It's the warmth of human presence that transforms lonely afternoons into meaningful ones.

### How will I know caregivers are trustworthy?

Safety is our foundation, not an afterthought. We're building thorough verification into everything we do because your loved one's safety matters to us as much as it does to you. Every caregiver on iCare will complete our verification process before they can connect with families. While we're not sharing all the operational details yet, we're designing this with your peace of mind at the centre. More information will be available when we launch.

### Can I choose my own caregiver?

Absolutely. That's one of the key differences between iCare and traditional agencies. You'll be able to see profiles of verified companions in your area, learn about their interests and experience, and make an informed choice about who feels like the right match for your loved one. You're not just assigned whoever happens to be available.

### What if I need personal care, not just companionship?

We're starting with companionship services at launch because connection is the foundation of good care. However, personal care support is on our roadmap for the future. If you join the waitlist, you'll hear from us as we expand into additional services. In the meantime, companionship can make a meaningful difference for many families whose loved ones primarily need company and social connection.

### How much will it cost?

We're focused on making quality companionship accessible for families while ensuring caregivers are fairly compensated for their work. We're finalising the details around pricing and will share transparent information closer to launch. Families on our waitlist will be among the first to know when pricing details are available.

## For Caregivers

### Do I need qualifications to join?

For companionship care, what matters most is who you are as a person. Are you patient, kind, and reliable? Can you make people feel heard and valued? Do you treat elderly adults with dignity and respect? Those qualities are what make a great companion caregiver. While formal care qualifications are welcome, they're not required for providing companionship. We're looking for people who genuinely care about connecting with others.

### Can I set my own hours and rates?

Yes. You'll have control over your availability, the areas you're willing to travel to, and the type of companionship you want to provide. This is your practice, and you decide what works for your life. You're not fitting into someone else's rota or being told where to be and when. We're building this to give caregivers genuine flexibility and control.

### How does the platform work?

You'll create a profile that shows who you are and what you offer. Families seeking companionship will be able to find your profile and reach out. You'll see their requests and decide which ones feel like the right fit. When you accept an arrangement, you'll provide companionship on terms that work for both you and the family. Over time, you'll build a reputation based on the quality care you provide. We're designing this to be simple and straightforward while supporting you to build meaningful relationships.

### What support will I receive?

You'll be part of a community of caregivers who share your values. We're building resources, guidance, and support into the platform to help you succeed. While you'll have the independence to run your own practice, you won't be alone. More details about the specific support we'll provide will be shared closer to launch.

### How do I get paid?

We're building a straightforward payment system into the platform. You'll be paid for the companionship you provide, and we're committed to making sure caregivers are fairly compensated for their work. The specific details about how payments work, timing, and processing will be shared when we launch. What's important to know now is that fairness and transparency are core values in everything we're designing.

## Waitlist Questions

### Why should I join the waitlist?

Joining the waitlist means you'll be among the first to access iCare when we launch. You'll get priority access, regular updates on our progress, and the opportunity to help shape what we build. Waitlist members are our founding community, and your input matters to us. Whether you're a family seeking care or a caregiver looking for better opportunities, being on the waitlist puts you at the front of the queue.

### What happens after I sign up?

Once you join the waitlist, you'll receive a confirmation email welcoming you to the community. From there, we'll send you regular updates sharing our progress, explaining features we're building, and asking for your feedback on what matters most to you. There's no obligation and no commitment - you're simply expressing interest in a better way forward.

### Will I get priority when you launch?

Yes. Waitlist members will be the first to be invited to join iCare when we launch. We're building this for the families and caregivers who believe, like we do, that there's a better way to approach companionship care. Being on the waitlist means you'll be first in line when we're ready.

### How can I stay updated?

The best way to stay informed is to join our waitlist. You'll receive regular email updates about our progress. You can also subscribe to our Care Guidance newsletter, which provides practical advice for families navigating elderly care challenges while we're building the platform. We share updates on social media as well, but email is the most reliable way to hear from us directly.
