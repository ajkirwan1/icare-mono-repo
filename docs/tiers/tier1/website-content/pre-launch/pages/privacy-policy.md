## What Information We Collect

Right now, we only collect information when you join our waitlist or subscribe to our newsletter.

**When you join the waitlist, we collect**:
- Your email address (required)
- Your name (optional)
- Your role preference: family member or caregiver (optional)

**When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect**:
- Your email address (required)
- Your name (optional)

We don't collect any other information at this stage. No phone numbers, addresses, medical information, or care needs.

**How we collect it**: You provide this information directly when you fill out a signup form on our website.

**Cookies**: We use essential cookies to make our website work. We'll ask for your consent before using any marketing or analytics cookies.

---

## Why We Collect This Information

We collect your email address so we can:

**For waitlist members**:
- Send you updates about our launch progress
- Notify you when the platform is ready
- Give you priority access before we open to the public
- Share exclusive updates about what we're building

**For newsletter subscribers**:
- Send you weekly care guidance content
- Share practical advice about elderly care and companionship
- Provide research and resources to help families

**Optional information** (name and role preference) helps us personalise our emails and send you content that's more relevant to you.

**Legal basis**: We process your data based on your consent. When you tick the consent box and submit the form, you're giving us permission to contact you.

---

## How We Use Your Information

We will only use your email address to send you the specific emails you signed up for.

**We will NOT**:
- Sell your email address to anyone
- Share your information with third parties for their marketing
- Send you spam or unrelated marketing
- Use your data for purposes you didn't agree to

**We WILL**:
- Send you the waitlist updates or newsletter content you requested
- Use a trusted email service provider to deliver emails
- Keep your information secure
- Respect your right to unsubscribe at any time

---

## Who We Share Your Information With

We use a trusted email service provider to send emails. This provider processes your email address on our behalf.

**Our email provider**:
- Has signed a Data Processing Agreement with us
- Is required to protect your data
- Can only use your data to send emails on our behalf
- Cannot use your data for their own purposes

We don't share your information with anyone else. If this changes, we'll update this policy and let you know.

---

## How Long We Keep Your Information

**Waitlist members**: We keep your information until you become a platform user, or for a maximum of 24 months if you don't engage with our emails. If you don't open or click any of our emails for 12 months, we'll send you a re-engagement email asking if you want to stay on the list.

**Newsletter subscribers**: We keep your information as long as you're subscribed. You can unsubscribe anytime.

**After you unsubscribe**: We remove your information within 30 days. We keep your email address on a suppression list to make sure we don't accidentally add you back to our list.

**If you request deletion**: We delete your information within 30 days of your request.

## Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

**Access your data**: Ask us what information we hold about you.

**Correct your data**: Ask us to update or correct your information if it's wrong.

**Delete your data**: Ask us to delete your information completely (also called the "right to be forgotten").

**Withdraw consent**: Unsubscribe from emails at any time. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link.

**Object to processing**: Tell us to stop using your information.

**Data portability**: Ask us to give you a copy of your information in a common format.

**To exercise any of these rights**, email us at privacy@icare-app.co.uk. We'll respond within 30 days.

**To unsubscribe quickly**, click the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we send you.

## How We Protect Your Information

We take data security seriously:

- We use a reputable, GDPR-compliant email service provider
- We don't store payment information (we don't take payments yet)
- We limit who has access to your information

No system is 100% secure, but we do everything reasonable to protect your data.
