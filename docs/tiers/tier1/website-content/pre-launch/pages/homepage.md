## 2. The Problem: Why This Matters

Loneliness is one of the most serious challenges facing older adults in the UK today. According to Age UK, 1.4 million elderly people are often lonely. Research from the NHS shows that chronic loneliness affects health as much as smoking 15 cigarettes a day. It increases the risk of dementia, heart disease, and early death. This isn't just about feeling a bit isolated. This is a serious health concern that too many families face in silence.

If you're worried about a parent, relative, or spouse who seems increasingly withdrawn or isolated, you're right to be concerned. You've noticed they don't see friends as often. The weekly groups they used to attend have been dropped. Phone calls are shorter, less animated. When you visit, there's a flatness you can't quite name. You want to help, but you're already stretched thin between work, your own family, and life's demands.

Many families in your situation feel guilty. They feel like they should be doing more, visiting more often, calling more frequently. But the truth is, you can't be everywhere at once. You can't fill every hour of loneliness, no matter how much you love them. And here's what's important to understand: getting support isn't a sign of failure. It's an act of love.

The challenge is finding that support. Traditional care agencies feel too clinical, too impersonal. You're not looking for someone to rush through a 15-minute visit to tick a box. You're looking for someone who will actually connect with your loved one. Someone trustworthy who can bring conversation, companionship, and warmth into their day. Someone who makes them feel like a person, not a task.

Finding that kind of person is harder than it should be. Most families spend hours searching online, asking friends, posting in local groups, only to feel overwhelmed by options that don't quite fit. Too expensive. Too formal. Too uncertain. You want someone you can trust, but you don't know where to start.

You deserve a better way. So does your loved one.

## 3. Our Vision: A Better Way Forward

We're building iCare to be that better way. A place where families can find trusted, caring companions for elderly relatives, and where caregivers who value meaningful relationships over rushed visits can find rewarding work.

This isn't another care agency. It isn't a job board where you're left to figure everything out alone. We're building a platform designed around trust, quality, and real human connection. A service that makes it genuinely easier for families to find the right person, not just any person.

We're starting with companionship services because connection matters most. Companionship is about spending quality time together. It's going for a walk in the park, sharing a cup of tea and conversation, helping with a jigsaw puzzle, watching a favourite programme together, or simply sitting in comfortable company. It's the warmth of human presence that transforms a lonely afternoon into a meaningful one.

We believe elderly adults deserve to be treated with dignity and respect. We believe caregivers deserve to be valued for the skilled, meaningful work they do. And we believe families deserve transparency, choice, and peace of mind when seeking support for someone they love.

Right now, we're building. We're getting this right from the ground up. We're creating something different, something better, and we want you to be part of it from the beginning.

## 5. For Caregivers: Work That Matters

If you're a caregiver frustrated by rushed visits, poor pay, and being treated like a number, we're building iCare for you too.

You got into care work because you wanted to help people. You wanted to make a difference in someone's day. But if you've worked for traditional care agencies, you know the reality often doesn't match that vision. Fifteen-minute visits where you're expected to do too much in too little time. Schedules that change constantly. Travel time you don't get paid for. Feeling undervalued and unseen. It's exhausting, and it's not what drew you to this work in the first place.

We're building a different model. One where caregivers are respected as the skilled professionals you are. One where you have the flexibility to set your own hours and choose who you work with. One where you can build real relationships with the people you support, not just rush through visits.

Companionship care is meaningful work. It's about being present with someone. Listening to their stories. Sharing in their interests. Bringing warmth and connection into their life. That kind of work deserves respect, and caregivers who provide it deserve to be valued.

If you join the caregiver waitlist, you'll be part of our founding community. You'll get early access when we launch. We want caregivers who share our values at the heart of what we build.

## 6. How It Will Work: Simple, Human-Centred

We're designing iCare to be as simple as possible while maintaining the quality and safety that matters most.

**For families**, you'll be able to describe what you're looking for and see profiles of verified companions in your area. You'll choose who feels like the right match. You'll arrange visits that work for your loved one's needs and preferences. You'll have peace of mind knowing that safety and verification are built into the process.

**For caregivers**, you'll create a profile that shows who you are and what you offer. Families will be able to find you. You'll have control over your schedule and the work you take on. You'll build relationships with the people you support, not just tick boxes on a rota.

**For everyone**, the focus will be on connection, trust, and quality. We're building this to feel personal and human, not like another impersonal platform. Real people helping real people.

We're working hard to get this right. In the coming months, we'll be launching to our waitlist community first. If you want to be among the first to access what we're building, join the waitlist today.
