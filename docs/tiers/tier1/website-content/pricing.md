## How Pricing Works

### Section Headline
**Understanding iCare's Pricing Model**

### The Basics

**Caregivers Set Their Own Rates**:

Every caregiver chooses their hourly rate based on:
- Their experience and qualifications
- Local market rates
- Services they offer
- Their availability

Typical hourly rates range from **12-25 GBP per hour** depending on the caregiver's background and location (London/Southeast often higher).

---

**You See the Exact Cost Before You Book**:

When you view a caregiver's profile or request a booking, you see:
- Their hourly rate (e.g., 18 GBP/hour)
- Your chosen duration (e.g., 3 hours)
- Total cost (e.g., 54 GBP)

What you see is what you pay. **No hidden fees for families.**

---

**Platform Commission (Caregiver Side)**:

iCare charges a **10% commission** on each booking. This commission is paid by the caregiver, not the family.

The commission covers:
- Payment processing (Stripe fees)
- ID and right to work verification infrastructure
- Safeguarding and admin oversight
- Platform development and maintenance
- 24/7 customer support
- Escrow system operation

**Example for Caregivers**:
- Caregiver sets rate: 20 GBP/hour
- 3-hour booking: 60 GBP
- Platform commission (10%): 6 GBP
- Caregiver receives: 54 GBP

## For Families: What You Pay

### Section Headline
**Complete Transparency for Care Receivers and Families**

### What You Pay

**Just the Caregiver's Rate × Hours Booked**:
- No booking fees
- No service charges to families
- No hidden costs
- No surge pricing

**Payment Methods Accepted**:
- Credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Debit card (Visa Debit, Mastercard Debit)

All cards processed securely through Stripe.

---

### When Payment is Taken

**Booking Request**:
When you request a booking, your card is **authorized** (checked that funds are available) but **not charged yet**.

**Booking Acceptance**:
When the caregiver accepts your booking request, payment is **captured** (actually charged to your card) and held in **escrow** (secure holding, not yet released to caregiver).

**Booking Completion**:
When the caregiver marks the booking as complete, payment is **released** from escrow to the caregiver (within 2 business days).

**Why Escrow?**:
- You don't pay until care is actually confirmed
- Caregiver is guaranteed payment once care is complete
- Platform can issue refunds if there's a problem
- Fair to both parties

---

### Minimum Booking Duration

**2 Hours Minimum**:

All bookings have a minimum duration of 2 hours.

**Why?**:
- Covers caregiver travel time
- Ensures meaningful care time (not rushed)
- Typical market standard for companionship care
- Allows for quality interaction

---

### Cancellation Policy and Refunds

**More than 24 Hours Before Booking**:
- **Full refund**
- No questions asked
- Caregiver notified, slot opens up for others

**Less than 24 Hours Before Booking**:
- **50% refund**
- Partial compensation to caregiver for short-notice cancellation
- Caregiver may have already declined other bookings

**Family No-Show** (Not home when caregiver arrives):
- **No refund**
- Caregiver has traveled and honored their commitment
- Payment released to caregiver

**Caregiver No-Show**:
- **Full refund**
- Caregiver may face suspension or ban
- Report immediately to support team

**Care Not Provided as Agreed**:
- Contact support immediately
- We investigate all claims
- Partial or full refunds issued where appropriate

---

### No Off-Platform Payments

**Platform Requirement**:

All payments **must** go through iCare. Off-platform payments (cash, bank transfer, PayPal) are **prohibited**.

**Why?**:
- On-platform payments are protected by escrow
- You can get refunds if something goes wrong
- Safeguarding: we can track transactions for exploitation concerns
- Fair to everyone: caregivers can't bypass the platform that verified them

**If a Caregiver Asks for Cash or Direct Payment**:

🚨 **Report immediately** - This is a serious violation and grounds for caregiver suspension.

Report via:
- In-app "Report Concern" button
- Email: safeguarding@icare.co.uk
- Phone: [SUPPORT LINE]

## For Caregivers: Earnings and Commission

### Section Headline
**Fair Pay for Professional Caregivers**

### How You Set Your Rate

**You're in Control**:

You decide your hourly rate based on:
- Your experience (years in care work)
- Your qualifications (Care Certificate, NVQ, nursing background, etc.)
- Local market rates (urban vs. rural, London vs. regions)
- Services you offer
- Your availability (caregivers with limited availability may charge more)

**Typical Range**: 12-25 GBP/hour

**Lower End (12-18 GBP/hour)**:
- Newer to professional care work
- Companionship-only focus
- Rural or lower-cost-of-living areas
- High availability

**Mid-Range (18-22 GBP/hour)**:
- Several years' experience
- Some qualifications or training
- Urban areas
- Good reviews and ratings

**Upper End (22-25+ GBP/hour)**:
- Extensive experience
- Professional qualifications
- London/Southeast
- Specialized skills or languages
- Outstanding reviews
- Limited availability (high demand)

---

### Platform Commission

**10% of Each Booking**:

iCare charges **10% commission** on every completed booking.

**What the Commission Covers**:
- Stripe payment processing fees
- ID verification infrastructure (Stripe Identity)
- Right to work verification
- Admin approval and oversight
- Safeguarding systems (24/7 incident reporting, SAB liaison)
- Escrow payment system
- Platform development and maintenance
- Customer support
- Marketing to bring families to the platform

**How It's Calculated**:

| Your Rate | Booking Value | Commission (10%) | You Receive (90%) |
|-----------|---------------|------------------|-------------------|
| 15 GBP/hour | 30 GBP (2 hours) | 3 GBP | 27 GBP |
| 18 GBP/hour | 54 GBP (3 hours) | 5.40 GBP | 48.60 GBP |
| 20 GBP/hour | 80 GBP (4 hours) | 8 GBP | 72 GBP |
| 25 GBP/hour | 125 GBP (5 hours) | 12.50 GBP | 112.50 GBP |

Commission is deducted automatically before payout. You don't calculate anything - it's handled by the platform.

---

### When You Get Paid

**Automatic Payout Process**:

1. **Booking Confirmed**: Family pays, funds held in escrow
2. **You Provide Care**: Complete the companionship visit as agreed
3. **Mark as Complete**: In the app, mark the booking complete
4. **Payment Released**: Within **2 business days**, money arrives in your bank account

**No Invoicing Required**:
- Completely automatic
- No chasing families for payment
- No awkward money conversations
- Reliable, on-time payments

**Bank Account Setup**:
During registration, you connect your bank account via Stripe Connect (secure, industry-standard system). All payouts go directly to this account.

---

### Tax Responsibilities

**You Are Self-Employed**:

As a self-employed professional, you are responsible for:

**Income Tax**:
- Register with HMRC for Self Assessment
- Declare all income from iCare bookings
- Pay income tax on your earnings
- File annual tax return by January 31st

**National Insurance**:
- Pay Class 2 National Insurance (if earnings above threshold)
- Pay Class 4 National Insurance on profits
- Thresholds and rates vary annually - check HMRC website

**Record Keeping**:
- Keep records of all income (your iCare earnings dashboard helps)
- Track business expenses (mileage, phone, supplies, training, DBS cost, etc.)
- Retain records for at least 5 years

**What iCare Does NOT Do**:
- We do NOT deduct tax from your payments
- We do NOT pay employer National Insurance
- We do NOT provide employment benefits (holiday pay, sick pay, pension)
- We do NOT file tax returns on your behalf

**Tax Resources**:
- HMRC Self Assessment: gov.uk/self-assessment-tax-returns
- HMRC Helpline for Self-Employed: 0300 200 3310
- We recommend consulting an accountant or tax advisor

**Your Earnings Dashboard**:
Your iCare dashboard provides complete payment history, which you can use for tax record keeping.

## FAQs: Pricing and Payments

### For Families

#### Are there any hidden fees?

No. You pay exactly the caregiver's hourly rate × number of hours. No booking fees, no service charges, no surprises.

---

#### Why do caregivers pay commission instead of families?

This model is common in professional service marketplaces. It keeps costs transparent for families (you see exactly what you pay) while ensuring caregivers who benefit from verification, payment processing, and booking infrastructure contribute to platform costs.

---

#### Can I pay the caregiver directly to avoid fees?

No. All payments must go through the platform. Off-platform payments are prohibited and should be reported immediately. On-platform payments protect you with escrow, refund policies, and transaction records for safeguarding.

---

#### What if I need to cancel?

**More than 24 hours before**: Full refund
**Less than 24 hours**: 50% refund
**No-show**: No refund

See full cancellation policy above.

---

#### When am I charged?

Your card is authorized when you request a booking, but not charged until the caregiver accepts. Payment is then held in escrow until care is completed.

---

#### What payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Stripe. We don't accept cash, cheques, or bank transfers.

---

#### Is my payment information secure?

Yes. All payments processed through Stripe with bank-level encryption. Your card details are never shared with caregivers or stored on our servers.

---

#### Can I get a receipt for tax purposes?

Yes. All booking confirmations include payment receipts. You can download payment history from your account dashboard.

---

### For Caregivers

#### How much commission do you charge?

**10%** of each completed booking. This covers payment processing, verification infrastructure, safeguarding systems, and platform maintenance.

---

#### When do I get paid?

Within **2 business days** of marking a booking as complete. Payouts are automatic - no invoicing required.

---

#### How do I get paid?

Direct bank transfer via Stripe Connect. You connect your bank account during registration, and all payouts go directly there.

---

#### Are there any hidden fees for caregivers?

No. Just the 10% commission per booking. No monthly subscription, no listing fees, no payout fees.

---

#### How do I set my hourly rate?

You choose your rate during profile setup. You can change it anytime, but existing booking requests honor the rate advertised when the request was made. Consider your experience, qualifications, and local market rates (typically 12-25 GBP/hour).

---

#### Can I charge different rates for different services?

Not at this stage. You set one hourly rate that applies to all your services (companionship, housework, shopping, meal prep). In future tiers, we may add service-specific pricing.

---

#### Can I negotiate rates with families?

No. Your advertised rate is what families pay. This keeps pricing transparent and prevents awkward negotiations. If your rate isn't attracting bookings, you can adjust it in your profile settings.

---

#### What if a family doesn't pay?

This can't happen. Families pay when they book (payment held in escrow). You're guaranteed payment when you complete the booking. If there's a dispute, we investigate and ensure fair outcomes.

---

#### Do I pay tax on my earnings?

Yes. You're self-employed, so you must register with HMRC for Self Assessment, declare all income, and pay income tax and National Insurance. iCare does not deduct tax - that's your responsibility. We recommend consulting an accountant.

---

#### Can families tip me?

Not through the platform at this stage. If a family wants to show extra appreciation, they can leave a glowing review or book you again - that's the best "tip" for building your reputation and attracting more bookings.
