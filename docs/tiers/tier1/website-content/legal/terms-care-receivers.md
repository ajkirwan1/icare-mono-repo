## 2. Definitions

**"Care Receiver"**: An individual receiving companionship or light assistance services, or their authorized representative.

**"Caregiver"**: A self-employed independent professional who offers companionship and light assistance services through the Platform.

**"Introduction Agency"**: A business model where the Platform connects self-employed professionals with service users but does NOT employ caregivers or provide care services directly.

**"Booking"**: A request for companionship or light assistance services made through the Platform.

**"Services"**: The Platform's marketplace services, including caregiver search, booking facilitation, payment processing, and verification.

**"Companionship Services"**: Non-regulated care services at Tier 1, including companionship visits, light housework, shopping assistance, and meal preparation. Does NOT include personal care (washing, dressing, toileting).

## 3. Platform Model: Introduction Agency **[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]**

### 3.1 What the Platform Is

The Platform operates as an **Introduction Agency**. We are a technology marketplace that:
- Connects care receivers with self-employed companion caregivers
- Facilitates bookings and payments between users
- Verifies caregiver identity, right to work, and background checks
- Provides safeguarding infrastructure and reporting tools
- Enables quality signals through reviews and ratings

### 3.2 What the Platform Is NOT

The Platform is NOT:
- A care service provider
- An employment agency (caregivers are NOT our employees)
- CQC-registered (we do NOT provide regulated care activities)
- A clinical care provider (we do NOT create or manage care plans)
- A guarantor of care quality or outcomes

### 3.3 Caregiver Independence

**CRITICAL ACKNOWLEDGMENT**: Caregivers on the Platform are **self-employed independent professionals**, NOT employees of the Platform. Caregivers:
- Set their own hourly rates
- Control their own availability
- Have complete discretion to accept or decline bookings
- Determine how they deliver care services
- Are responsible for their own tax, insurance, and business obligations

The Platform does NOT control how caregivers deliver services.

### 3.4 Your Relationship with Caregivers

When you book a caregiver through the Platform:
- You are entering into a direct service agreement with that caregiver
- You are NOT engaging the Platform to provide care services
- The caregiver is responsible for the quality and delivery of care
- The Platform facilitates the connection and payment only

**Reference**: Care Act 2014, Health and Social Care Act 2008, Consumer Rights Act 2015

## 4. Tier 1 Service Scope **[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]**

### 4.1 Services Available at Tier 1

At Tier 1, the Platform connects care receivers with caregivers for **companionship and light assistance services ONLY**:
- Companionship visits and social interaction
- Light housework (tidying, dusting, laundry)
- Shopping and errands
- Meal preparation
- Transportation (if caregiver offers and has appropriate insurance)

### 4.2 Services NOT Available at Tier 1

The following services are NOT available at Tier 1:
- Personal care (washing, bathing, dressing, toileting assistance)
- Medical or clinical care
- Medication administration
- Specialist dementia care
- Live-in care
- Overnight care

**If you require personal care services**, these will be available at Tier 2 (future). Please join the waitlist at [WAITLIST_URL].

### 4.3 Regulatory Classification

Companionship services at Tier 1 are NOT "regulated activities" under the Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006. This is why:
- DBS checks are voluntary (not mandatory) at Tier 1
- CQC registration is not required for the Platform
- Personal care services (which ARE regulated) are not yet available

When personal care services launch at Tier 2, mandatory DBS checks and enhanced verification will apply.

**Reference**: Safeguarding Vulnerable Groups Act 2006, Section 5

## 6. Booking Process

### 6.1 Booking Requests

When you request a booking:
1. You search for caregivers by location, availability, and service type
2. You select a caregiver and choose date, time, and duration
3. You authorize payment (held in escrow until booking completion)
4. The caregiver has 24 hours to accept or decline
5. If accepted, contact details are exchanged and booking is confirmed
6. If declined or not responded to within 24 hours, your payment authorization is cancelled

### 6.2 No Guarantee of Availability

The Platform does NOT guarantee that:
- Any specific caregiver will accept your booking request
- Any caregiver will be available at your preferred time
- You will find a caregiver in your area

Caregivers are self-employed and have complete discretion to accept or decline any booking.

### 6.3 Booking Confirmation

A booking is only confirmed when:
- The caregiver explicitly accepts your request
- Payment authorization is successful
- You receive booking confirmation from the Platform

### 6.4 Special Requests

You may include special requests (maximum 500 characters) in your booking. However:
- Special requests are for information only
- Caregivers are NOT obligated to fulfill special requests outside Tier 1 service scope
- Requests for personal care services will be flagged and the booking may be cancelled
