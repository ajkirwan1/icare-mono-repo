## 8. Data Retention **[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]**

### 8.1 How Long We Keep Your Data

| Data Category | Retention Period | Reason |
|---------------|------------------|--------|
| **Active accounts** | While account is active + 7 years | Legal claims period, contractual obligations |
| **Deleted accounts** | 30 days (pseudonymized) then 7 years (anonymized) | Allow account recovery period, then legal retention |
| **Booking records** | 7 years after booking completion | Financial records (HMRC requirement: 6 years), safeguarding (7 years) |
| **Payment transactions** | 6 years after transaction | HMRC legal requirement |
| **Safeguarding incident reports** | 7 years minimum | Care Act 2014 requirement, legal claims |
| **Verification documents** | While account active + 3 years | Right to work re-verification, audit trail |
| **Audit logs** | 3 years | GDPR accountability requirement |
| **Marketing consents** | Until consent withdrawn | PECR requirement |

### 8.2 Account Deletion

When you delete your account:
- Your profile is immediately removed from caregiver search
- Personal data is pseudonymized after 30 days (allow recovery period)
- Data is fully anonymized or deleted after 7 years (except where legal retention required)
- Booking records are retained for legal/financial compliance (anonymized after 7 years)
- Safeguarding reports are retained for 7 years minimum

### 8.3 Right to Erasure Limitations

You have the right to request erasure ("right to be forgotten"), BUT we may refuse if:
- Retention is required by law (HMRC financial records, safeguarding reports)
- Necessary for legal claims (limitation period: 6-7 years)
- Required for compliance with legal obligations (Care Act 2014)

**Reference**: UK GDPR Article 17 (right to erasure)

**2. Right to Rectification**:

**4. Right to Restriction of Processing**:
- Request temporary restriction of processing
- Available if accuracy disputed, processing unlawful, or legal claim pending
- We respond within 1 month

**5. Right to Data Portability**:
- Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- Transfer data to another service provider
- Applies to data you provided under contract or consent
- We respond within 1 month

**6. Right to Object**:
- Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- Object to direct marketing (we will stop immediately)
- We respond within 1 month

**7. Right to Withdraw Consent**:

- Does NOT affect lawfulness of processing before withdrawal

**8. Right to Complain to ICO**:
- Complain to Information Commissioner's Office if unhappy
- ICO website: https://ico.org.uk
- ICO helpline: 0303 123 1113

## 10. Data Security **[LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED]**

### 10.1 How We Protect Your Data

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

**Technical Measures**:
- **Encryption at rest**: All data encrypted in database (AES-256)
- **Encryption in transit**: HTTPS/TLS throughout Platform
- **Password security**: Passwords hashed using bcrypt (never stored plain text)
- **Multi-factor authentication**: Available for admin accounts
- **Access controls**: Role-based access control (RBAC)
- **Audit logging**: All data access logged for accountability
- **Regular security testing**: Penetration testing and vulnerability scanning
- **Intrusion detection**: Monitoring for unauthorized access

**Organizational Measures**:
- **Staff training**: Data protection and safeguarding training for all staff
- **Data minimization**: Only collect data necessary for service provision
- **Privacy by Design**: Data protection built into system architecture
- **Data Protection Officer**: DPO oversight of data practices
- **Incident response plan**: Procedures for data breaches
- **Vendor security**: Third-party processors contractually required to protect data

### 10.2 Data Breach Notification

If we experience a data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms:
- We will notify the ICO within 72 hours
- We will notify you without undue delay if high risk
- We will describe the nature of the breach, likely consequences, and mitigation measures

**Reference**: UK GDPR Article 33-34 (breach notification)
